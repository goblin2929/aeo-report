Executive Summary

Period in Review: a broad demand rebound, led by the Indonesian game PDPs

Combined clicks rebounded to 154,082 (+44.86% vs the prior week), recovering strongly after the early September normalization and setting a new high. The gain is broad and organic led: brand +33.5%, non-branded +58.2% and the PDP page cut +65.7% combined, with positions holding across markets:

Brand +29.2%, non-branded +57.9%, PDP +79.4%; overall position improved 5.64 to 5.55. The Roblox voucher PDP also improved to position 2.3.

  • Malaysia +26.3%, up on a tiny base, position improving.
  • The pattern is a broad demand recovery after the prior week's dip, with the Indonesian product pages doing the heavy lifting and positions stable to improving.
