Executive Summary

Period in Review: the first full week after the gogogo.com move, brand up and non-branded easing while Google reindexes

Combined clicks rose to 161,924 (+5.08% vs the prior week). Indonesia is now measured across both domains (gogogo.id plus gogogo.com/id-id), since the site moved on 21 September:

  • Indonesia +7.8%, with brand +52.8% as users searched for the site after the move ("gogogo" 31,858 to 51,519) and non-branded -26.3%, off the peak week and while the new URLs reindex (still above the 7 to 13 September level). gogogo.com/id-id already took 10,490 clicks.
  • The Indonesian PDP figure (+51.1%) is inflated by branded homepage traffic that Google briefly routed to /p/highlighted and the Free Fire Max PDP while it dropped the old homepage. This is a reindexing effect, not product demand.
  • Philippines -6.6% off the peak week with position improving (4.28 to 4.03); Malaysia +18.9% on a small base.
  • The main thing to watch is the new gogogo.com/id-id pages settling on the non-branded head terms. Details in the post migration analysis.

Indonesia +7.8% across both domains: brand searches surged while non-branded eased during reindexing

This is the first full week on the new domain. Counting gogogo.id and gogogo.com/id-id together, Indonesian clicks rose 7.8% (124,771 to 134,464), and gogogo.com/id-id already carried 10,490 of them. Branded clicks jumped +52.8% (53,771 to 82,171), led by "gogogo" (31,858 to 51,519) and "gogogo id" (5,654 to 10,020), as users searched for the site after the domain change.

Non-branded clicks eased 26.3% (71,000 to 52,293). Part of that is the prior week being an exceptional peak (this week is still above the 7 to 13 September level of 44,955), and part is the new URLs still being reindexed and sitting lower on the head terms: "top up ff" 11,604 to 8,779 (position 5.1 to 5.4), "top up roblox" 4,347 to 2,860 and "top up ml" 5,536 to 4,669. The blog eased 27.4% as blog URLs hand over to the new domain.

Read the PDP figure with care. The PDP page cut shows +51.1%, but most of that increase is brand homepage traffic shown on /p/highlighted and the Free Fire Max PDP while Google reindexes the new homepage (see the note under Top PDP Pages). The main Free Fire PDP eased from 23,196 to 12,504 clicks at a steady position (4.8 to 4.9).

Why /p/highlighted and the Free Fire Max PDP jumped: a normal effect of the migration

While Google reindexes the Indonesian homepage from gogogo.id to gogogo.com/id-id, the homepage is temporarily not the page it shows for brand searches such as "gogogo". Until the new homepage takes over, Google shows the most relevant other Gogogo pages in its place. This week that was /p/highlighted (22,372 clicks, 18,233 of them from "gogogo", shown from 25 September) and the Free Fire Max PDP (16,227 clicks, mostly from "gogogo", shown on 24 and 25 September).

This is a common situation while a migration is in progress. The clicks on these two pages are brand homepage traffic rather than new product demand, and they should move back to the gogogo.com/id-id homepage as reindexing completes. Recovery always takes time, so we expect this to settle gradually over the coming weeks and will keep tracking it in each weekly report.
