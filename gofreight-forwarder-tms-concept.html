Trusted by Forwarders Worldwide

Freight forwarders know how to move cargo. The hard part is everything in between. 

Even with a traditional forwarder TMS in place, freight forwarders still face four daily gaps that slow decisions and impact performance across the business. 

Documents are scattered

Shipping documents arrive in many formats and tools. Teams still hunt for files, key in data, and match to the right shipment record.

Tasks are disconnected

Critical tasks sit in emails, chats, and separate trackers. Managers cannot see progress in one place and staff risk missing time-sensitive steps.

Email does not flow into the TMS

Pre-alerts, arrival notices, bookings, and rate approvals live in the inbox. Without inbox-level automation, people read, copy, and retype instead of moving shipments forward.

Rates are hidden

Spot rates and contract rates live in different systems, files, or email threads. Sales teams spend valuable time searching and verifying numbers before quoting, which slows responses and loses opportunities.

FAQ

What is GoFreight?

GoFreight is AI powered, cloud based freight forwarding software built specifically for forwarders, and it works as a complete forwarder TMS. It brings shipment operations, quoting, invoicing, CRM, tracking, and business analytics into one platform, accessible from anywhere in the world with an internet connection.

Is GoFreight available in my country?

Yes — GoFreight is a cloud-based platform that works anywhere in the world. You can run your freight forwarding operations from any country without installing local software or setting up on-premise servers. GoFreight currently has a strong presence across the United States, Mexico, China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Singapore, Indonesia, Vietnam, Malaysia, Thailand, and Cambodia, with forwarders in many other countries using the platform as well.

Can my back-office team in one country work with my operations team in another country using GoFreight?

Absolutely — this is one of GoFreight's core strengths. Because GoFreight is 100% cloud-based, your back-office team in, say, Vietnam or Indonesia can handle documentation and invoicing while your sales team in the US or Singapore manages clients — all working on the same live data, in real time, with zero lag. There's no need for file transfers, VPNs, or syncing between local servers. Everyone sees the same dashboard, the same shipment status, and the same customer records, no matter where they are.

What types of freight does GoFreight support?

GoFreight supports ocean import, ocean export, air import, and air export operations. Each mode has dedicated workflows, document templates, compliance filing tools, and automated task management built into the platform.

How does GoFreight help freight forwarders grow without hiring more staff?

GoFreight automates repetitive tasks like document filing, invoice generation, and customer notifications. Customers report handling 30% more shipments within 12 months, resolving exceptions 40% faster, and achieving up to 2x productivity — all with existing staff. Because the platform is cloud-based, you can scale your back-office to lower-cost countries without any infrastructure overhead.

Why is cloud-based software better than traditional freight forwarding software?

Traditional freight software requires local installation, IT maintenance, and on-premise servers — which means every office needs its own setup, and data doesn't flow between locations in real time. GoFreight's cloud-based approach eliminates all of that. Your entire team — whether they're in the US, Taiwan, Singapore, or Cambodia — logs into one platform with one set of data. Updates happen instantly, there's no version mismatch between offices, and you can onboard a new branch in a new country in days, not months.

Does GoFreight work for freight forwarders in Greater China and Taiwan?

Yes. GoFreight serves forwarders operating in China, Hong Kong, and Taiwan — some of the world's busiest trade hubs. Teams in Greater China can work side-by-side with back-office staff or partner offices anywhere in the world through GoFreight's cloud platform, with shared visibility on every shipment and document.

How does GoFreight improve customer service for freight forwarders?

GoFreight provides branded customer portals with real-time tracking, automated shipment alerts, and 24/7 document access. Your customers get a professional, always-on experience regardless of which country your team is operating from. Forwarders report up to 40% fewer customer support inquiries and a 35% improvement in satisfaction scores.

What is a freight forwarder TMS?

A freight forwarder TMS (transportation management system) is software built around how forwarders actually work: multimodal shipment execution, quoting and rate management, documentation, compliance filing, billing, and customer visibility in one system. GoFreight is AI powered freight forwarding software that covers this entire forwarder TMS workflow, from email intake to document processing to quoting.

How is a forwarder TMS different from a general TMS?

A general TMS is built for shippers and carriers to plan loads and manage fleets. A forwarder TMS is built for freight forwarders who sell and execute international shipments, so it centers on quoting, ocean and air documentation, compliance filings, partner settlement, and customer facing tracking. GoFreight covers this full forwarder workflow in one platform.
