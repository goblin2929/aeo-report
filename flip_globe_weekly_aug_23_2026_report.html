What happened to Globe: the hub recovered its ranking, reversing last week's slip

Globe clicks rose 5.2% (291 to 306) and the /id/globe hub recovered. The hub we flagged slipping last week (position 7.3 to 9.7 then) climbed back this week from 9.4 to 7.8 (impression-weighted, back toward page one), and its clicks rose from 56 to 78. Whatever caused the prior week's dip did not persist.

The Indonesian destination pages stayed the click engine (clicks 133 to 140), with their impression-weighted position easing slightly (5.81 to 6.44) on a demand mix shift rather than a ranking loss. The English destination pages remain deep on page two and three (16.79 to 16.30) and volatile, but they rank too low to move clicks either way (80 to 69).

GSC caveat: most Globe impressions are anonymized, so these are page-level figures; the hub recovery is the clear, reliable signal this week.

What happened to Converter: Indonesian mixed and slightly down, English up on small numbers

Converter Indonesian eased 5.9% (3,876 to 3,648) on a mixed picture, not a broad decline. The US Dollar amount pages gained (for example usd-usa-idr amount variants +22 and +17), while the Vietnamese Dong vnd-vnm-idr (-30) and the main US Dollar page (-30) eased on softer demand. The segment position held (7.09 to 7.12).

Converter English grew 6.2% (178 to 189) on small absolute numbers. The head page usd-usa-idr remains stuck around position 11 (page two), still the largest untapped ceiling. Pulling the head English pairs (usd to idr, sgd to idr) onto page one is where the English converter's growth sits.
