What drove the +5.79%: broad Indonesian demand now, English rankings banked for later

The gain is spread across the Indonesian destination pages, not one hero page. The largest contributors were /id/globe/india (+6 clicks), /id/globe/uae (+5), and /id/globe/turkey, /id/globe/thailand, /id/globe/singapore and /id/globe/pakistan (+3 each). Most of these already rank inside the top 8, and India and UAE actually added clicks while their position held flat, so this week's clicks are led by demand and CTR, not fresh ranking jumps.

The average-position improvement from 9.41 to 8.67 comes from a different set: the English destination pages climbing out of page two. The clearest movers (previous to current): /en/globe/germany 20.3 to 15.4, /en/globe/usa 19.8 to 16.2, the /en/globe hub 12.4 to 10.1, /en/globe/china 13.9 to 11.5, /en/globe/uae 20.6 to 17.1 and /en/globe/india 16.8 to 14.6. They each gained 2 to 5 positions, the destination heading work taking effect, but remain above the click converting line. This is banked ranking progress that has not yet surfaced as clicks, and the upside to watch as they cross into page one.

Watch list (demand, not ranking): /id/globe/philippines lost 10 clicks and /id/globe/malaysia lost 4, even though both held or improved their rank. This is a demand dip rather than a ranking loss.
