How the agencies were chosen

We started from the agencies that appear in Google and in AI answers for Singapore AEO, GEO and AI SEO agency searches.   5. Measurement. A written account of what is tracked and reported.

// MEASUREMENT

How AEO Results Are Measured

Cited vs recommended

An engine cites a brand when it uses the brand's page as a source, and recommends it when it names the brand in the answer. Ask for both, per engine.

Ranges over several runs

AI answers change between runs. Each buyer question should be checked several times and reported as a range.

Tied to pipeline

Visibility matters when it shows up in enquiries. Ask how AI referred visits and leads are reported next to citations.

More on this: how to measure AEO.

About AEO Agencies in Singapore

Which AEO agency is the best in Singapore?

It depends on what you need to see before you sign. Novastacks wrote this list and ranks itself first for its published method and named client results in AI search. Among the others, MediaOne publishes the most complete package (named client results, prices and daily tracking), Stridec is the most focused on Google AI Overviews, and Hashmeta names the most AI engines. The comparison table shows which of five signals each agency publishes, so you can weigh them yourself.

What is an AEO agency?

An AEO (answer engine optimization) agency works to get your brand cited and recommended inside the answers that AI engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews give. The work covers which buyer questions to win, pages written so engines can quote them, schema and crawler access, mentions on sources the engines trust, and tracking whether the engines name you.

Which agencies offer an answer engine readiness audit in Singapore?

Novastacks offers a free AEO audit you can run without a call. Hashmeta advertises a free AI visibility audit, Stridec a free AIO Visibility Checker and readiness audit, OOm a complimentary GEO and AI Overview impact report, and First Page Digital a free strategy session. AI Studio and MediaOne start their programmes with an audit of agreed prompts. When you shortlist, ask each one which engines and how many buyer questions the audit covers.

What is typically included in a monthly AEO retainer in Singapore?

Across the agencies on this list, a monthly retainer usually covers tracking a set of buyer questions in named AI engines, new or rewritten pages written to be quoted, schema and technical fixes, mentions on trusted third party sites, and a monthly report. At Novastacks the retainer adds a senior engagement lead who sets priorities and reviews every claim before it ships.

How much does AEO cost in Singapore?

Published prices on this list run from about $580 a month for a one engine starter package (MediaOne) to S$8,000 a month (Stridec's top tier), with project packages from $6,000 to $30,000+ at Hashmeta and a S$9,000 90 day foundation at LOOP. Entry packages often cover one or two engines, so check which engines each price includes. Novastacks quotes after the free audit, scoped to the gap it finds. More detail: AEO cost in Singapore.

Can an AEO agency guarantee ChatGPT citations?

No agency controls what a model says. OOm offers a 'rank or 3 months free' guarantee, but only if its own readiness score qualifies you. A credible agency commits to the work (pages, schema, mentions) and reports citation and recommendation movement every month, per engine.

Can I use a government grant for AEO in Singapore?

PSG, EDG and MRA ceased on 29 September 2026. From 30 September, Enterprise Singapore routes business grant support through the EDGE Grant (up to 70% for SMEs and up to 50% for non SMEs, depending on the activity, with up to S$100,000 a year). Some agency pages still advertise PSG, so ask the agency to show which EDGE activity covers the work before you budget on it.

Should I hire an AEO agency or build AEO in house?

Build in house if you already have content, technical SEO and analytics people with time to track AI answers every week. Hire an agency when you need that tracking, the page plan and the outreach running in parallel now, or when you want the playbook documented so your team can take it over later.

How is AEO different from SEO and GEO?

SEO earns a position in Google's results. AEO earns a citation or a recommendation inside an AI answer. GEO (generative engine optimization) is the name many Singapore agencies use for the same AI answer work; the labels overlap more than the work differs. Ask any agency which engines it tracks and what it reports, whatever it calls the service. Full comparison: AEO vs SEO.

How long until AEO shows results?

AI engines recrawl and retrieve faster than classic search. Citation movement is typically visible within 6 to 10 weeks of shipping the work; durable coverage across a full set of buyer questions builds over a quarter.
