Executive Summary

Period in Review: a steady week, with the heading work showing early signs

The Globe scope was roughly flat this week (Globe +0.35%, Converter ID -5.46%, Converter EN +9.85%, globe-related blogs +0.96%). The more useful signal is in the priority Globe pages, where the destination heading implementation is starting to show:

  • Priority Globe pages are trending up in rank. China has now recovered three weeks running (position 9.58 to 7.56 to 7.18) and its clicks jumped +350% this week; Philippines improved to position 5.30 (+75% clicks) and its English page to 7.40; English Malaysia improved 9.61 to 8.32 (+144% clicks). This is the early impact of the destination heading work, which we are now monitoring.
  • The Globe hub eased on demand. /id/globe fell -30.34% (62 clicks) with its position slipping 6.43 to 6.90, which pulled the overall Globe average down even as the country pages improved. Globe total held flat (+0.35%) because the country pages offset the hub.
  • Converter Indonesian softened on demand, not ranking. Clicks -5.46% with position flat at 6.85. Notably, the USD page impressions jumped +50.86% (316k to 476k) as USD-to-rupiah interest rose; it still ranks only 8.40, so the pool is growing but not yet converting.
  • Converter English continued its green shoot. +9.85% clicks and position improved 9.45 to 9.28; the English hub also recovered from last week's deep-query blip (position 33.82 back to 17.33), confirming that was noise. English remains the largest untapped ceiling.
  • Globe-related blogs held flat (+0.96%), with the SWIFT-code cluster still the standout opportunity (large pools at 0.2% to 0.7% CTR).

How to read this

This tracks the Globe and Currency target keywords against their target rank (Top 3 or 5), in the same format as the Gogogo monitor:

  • GSC Pos is our own 28-day impression-weighted average (29 June to 26 July 2026), from the highest-impression page ranking for that query. This drives the row colour.
  • Ahrefs Pos is shown as "Pending" this period because the Ahrefs connector was offline; it will be added on the next refresh, alongside the Ahrefs ranking page, exactly like the Gogogo monitor.

Row colour follows the GSC position: On target at or better than target, Close up to +3 worse, Far more than +3 worse, No data no GSC impressions. Scorecard: 6 on target, 12 close, 16 far, 6 with no GSC data. Saved as a time-series SSOT for quarter-over-quarter charting. 4. Technical hygiene: broken converter hreflang between the ID and EN versions (reminder)

Carried forward. The Indonesian and English converters use different path slugs (/id/kalkulator-mata-uang/ vs /en/currency-converter/), but the hreflang alternate tags only swap the /id/ and /en/ prefix and keep the wrong path, so each language points to a broken URL for the other. Verified: the ID page en-US alternate points to /en/kalkulator-mata-uang/... (404), and the EN page id-ID and x-default point to /id/currency-converter/... (301 redirect).

Current, ID page head (broken)
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="id-ID" href="https://flip.id/id/kalkulator-mata-uang/idr-mys-myr"/> <link rel="alternate" hreflang="en-US" href="https://flip.id/en/kalkulator-mata-uang/idr-mys-myr"/> <-- 404 (wrong path)
Fixed, ID page head
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="id-ID" href="https://flip.id/id/kalkulator-mata-uang/idr-mys-myr"/> <link rel="alternate" hreflang="en-US" href="https://flip.id/en/currency-converter/idr-mys-myr"/>

The rule: the alternate href must map the language prefix AND the path slug together (id + kalkulator-mata-uang, en + currency-converter), and always point to the final 200 URL. Apply across every currency-pair page; both language versions must list the same complete set of alternates.
