// CASE STUDY · FINTECH

International money transfer · Indonesia

More clicks from the same number of searches

The client, one of Indonesia's largest money transfer companies, asked us to grow its pages for sending money abroad. Each destination term is small on its own, and every destination page shared one template with the same generic FAQs.

We rewrote the titles and headings of its priority destination pages around how each destination is searched, live since late July. Destination page clicks rose 20% from May to August on flat impressions. Destination specific FAQs and FAQ structured data are delivered and rolling out.

// CASE STUDY · ENTERTAINMENT

Game top up marketplace · ID, PH, MY

Turning first page rankings into more clicks

The client, one of Indonesia's largest game top up marketplaces, also serving the Philippines and Malaysia, already ranked on the first page for its product terms, but its product titles left out the in game currency people search for.

We wrote a title standard, catalogued 459 product pages in one source of truth and ran an editorial programme. The retitled pages showed results within 17 days while rankings barely moved.
