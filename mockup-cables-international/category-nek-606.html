RFOU or BFOU: which one do you need?

The two families share the halogen free, mud resistant construction. The difference is how they behave in a fire.

RFOU (flame retardant)BFOU (fire resistant)
In a fireLimits the spread of flame along the cable runKeeps the circuit working for a set time during a fire
Test standardIEC 60332-3IEC 60331, plus IEC 60332-3
Typical useGeneral power, lighting, control and instrumentationEmergency power, fire and gas detection, shutdown and alarm systems
Screening options (instrumentation)(c) collective screen · (i) individual and collective(c) collective screen · (i) individual and collective

Questions buyers ask about NEK 606 cables

What is a NEK 606 cable?

A NEK 606 cable is a halogen free, flame retardant or fire resistant cable built to NEK 606, the Norwegian standard for offshore cables. Its sheath resists drilling mud and oil. It is used for power, control and instrumentation on fixed platforms, FPSOs, drilling units and offshore vessels.

Who stocks NEK 606 cable in Singapore?

Cables International holds NEK 606 power and instrumentation cables in its 150,000 sq ft Singapore facility. Stocked items leave within 24 hours of order, 7 days a week, for offshore projects around the world.

What is the difference between RFOU and BFOU cable?

RFOU cable is flame retardant: it limits the spread of fire along the cable (IEC 60332-3). BFOU cable is fire resistant: it keeps the circuit working for a set time during a fire (IEC 60331), so it is used for emergency and safety systems.

What do (i) and (c) mean in an instrumentation cable name?

(c) means the pairs share one collective screen. (i) means each pair has its own screen plus an overall screen, which protects sensitive signals from interference between pairs.

Can you supply NEK 606 cable for an urgent vessel or platform repair?

Yes. Send the cable type, size and length. Stocked items ship from Singapore within 24 hours of order, any day of the week, with test reports and documentation.
