Questions about RFOU cable

What is RFOU cable?

RFOU is a NEK 606 offshore power cable rated 0.6/1 kV. It has EPR insulation, a tinned copper braid and a halogen free sheath that resists drilling mud and oil. It is flame retardant and used for power, lighting and control on platforms, FPSOs and offshore vessels.

Is RFOU cable fire resistant?

No. RFOU is flame retardant: it limits the spread of fire along the cable (IEC 60332-3). For circuits that must keep working during a fire, choose BFOU, which is fire resistant to IEC 60331.
