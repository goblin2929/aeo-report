Executive Summary

Period in Review: the migration dip reached non-branded search; brand held and Google has accepted the move

Combined clicks fell to 111,233 (-31.31% vs the prior week). Indonesia, and from 28 September the Philippines, are measured across their old and new domains:

  • Indonesia -35.3%. Brand held (-6.7%), but non-branded fell 80.2% while Google re-evaluates the new gogogo.com/id-id URLs for the head terms ("top up ff" position 5.4 to 10.2, "top up ml" 7.0 to 22.7, "top up roblox" 3.6 to 16.9). The new domain now carries 37,884 of the 87,027 Indonesian clicks.

  • Philippines -11.9% in its first week on gogogo.com/en-ph, with PDPs up 6.8%; Malaysia -11.1% on a small base.
  • Details on the migration in the post migration analysis.

Indonesia -35.3%: the expected migration dip reached non-branded search, brand held

The second week on the new domain is where the migration dip shows. Counting gogogo.id and gogogo.com/id-id together, Indonesian clicks fell 35.3% (134,464 to 87,027); the new domain now carries 37,884 of them. Brand held (-6.7%, 82,171 to 76,679), but non-branded clicks fell 80.2% (52,293 to 10,348) while Google re-evaluates the new URLs for the head terms: "top up ff" 8,779 to 951 clicks (position 5.4 to 10.2), "top up ml" 4,669 to 61 (7.0 to 22.7) and "top up roblox" 2,860 to 112 (3.6 to 16.9). The PDP page cut fell 66.3% and the blog 87.9% for the same reason.

No technical blocker. We checked Google's own view with the Search Console URL Inspection tool on 7 October: the new homepage and the Free Fire, Mobile Legends and Roblox PDPs on gogogo.com/id-id are all "Submitted and indexed", crawlable, and Google has chosen the new URL as canonical, while the old gogogo.id URLs show as "Page with redirect". Google has accepted the move, so this is the ranking reassessment a domain migration goes through, and recovery takes time while the new URLs rebuild their positions.

Read the position figure with care. The Indonesian average position shows an improvement (5.34 to 4.12), but only because the non-branded queries, which rank lower, dropped out of the mix while branded queries (position 1 to 3) stayed. It is not a ranking gain.

Brand searches: Google is still showing other pages in place of the new homepage

As last week, Google is not yet showing the new homepage for every brand search and fills the gap with other Gogogo pages. /p/highlighted stopped receiving "gogogo" clicks after 30 September, which is progress. Since 25 September the old gogogo.id/register sign up page has taken its place: 32,740 clicks this week, 26,902 of them from "gogogo", peaking at 9,838 on 1 October. Brand searchers are landing on a registration form rather than the homepage.

The redirect itself is in place: gogogo.id/register returns a 308 to gogogo.com/id-id/register (checked live on 7 October). But at Google's last crawl of that URL on 3 October, Search Console still recorded it as an indexed page rather than a redirect, so Google has not processed it yet. Meanwhile the new gogogo.com/id-id homepage keeps taking over brand searches, from 1,708 clicks on 25 September to between 4,357 and 5,639 a day in October. This is a common situation while a migration is in progress and should resolve as Google recrawls.

Philippines -11.9% in its first week on gogogo.com/en-ph

The Philippine site moved to gogogo.com/en-ph on 28 September, so this is its first week across both domains. Clicks eased 11.9% (27,095 to 23,871), with gogogo.com/en-ph already carrying 6,953 of them. Brand eased 10.1% and non-branded 17.6%, while the PDP page cut grew 6.8% (6,127 to 6,546), led by the Mobile Legends First Recharge PDP (1,111 to 2,026). Google has accepted this move too: the new Philippine PDPs are indexed with the new URL as canonical and the old gogogo.game URLs show as redirects. The Philippine dip is so far much milder than Indonesia's, but its reindexing has only just started, so we will watch it closely next week.
