FAQ Improvement Impact (Flip Globe priority pages, launched 8 September)

The enriched FAQ is already pulling in destination specific queries and lifting target keywords

On 8 September we launched an improved FAQ on the priority Globe destination pages, adding real context about each destination rather than only the templated terms. Checking Search Console for the six days after launch (8 to 13 September) against the six days before (2 to 7 September), the early impact shows on two fronts.

1. More target keywords turned green. Globe target keywords on target rose from 7 to 11 this week. Four destination terms crossed onto target: transfer uang ke luar negeri (3.2 to 2.55), kirim uang ke luar negeri (4.0 to 2.85), transfer uang ke china (3.3 to 2.7) and transfer uang ke jepang (no data to position 1.0).

2. New destination specific queries appeared. Across the priority pages, 43 queries surfaced after launch that were not showing in the six days before, most of them the destination specific and how to send money intent the enriched FAQ targets, several already at strong positions. Examples below.
