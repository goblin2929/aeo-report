Executive Summary

Period in Review: the migration recovery has started, led by Indonesian non-branded search

Combined clicks rose to 122,081 (+36.37% vs the prior week). All three markets grew:

  • Indonesia +31.9%. Brand held steady (+4.8%) and non-branded search grew 249.3% (6,825 to 23,838) as the new gogogo.com/id-id product pages started ranking again for the head terms from about 17 October ("top up ml" position 20.6 to 7.8, "top up ff" 10.4 to 7.8).
  • Not fully recovered yet. Non-branded search is still under half its level in the last week before the move (52,293 clicks, 21 to 27 September), and "top up roblox" has not come back yet.
  • Brand searches now land on the homepage. Google is moving them from the root gogogo.com to gogogo.com/id-id (15,057 to 29,889 clicks), and the old register page now takes only a small share (1,219 clicks this week, against 33,090 in the week of 28 September).
  • Philippines +45.6%, with more brand searches ("gogogo ph" 15,724 to 22,393 clicks) and the Mobile Legends PDPs growing; Malaysia +124.4% on a small base.
  • Watch: two Indonesian Free Fire pages compete for "top up ff" (details in the Indonesia section).

Indonesia +31.9%: non-branded search is coming back on the new domain

Counting gogogo.id, gogogo.com/id-id and Indonesian searchers on the root gogogo.com together, clicks rose 31.9% (61,681 to 81,339). Brand held steady (+4.8%, 54,856 to 57,501). The growth came from non-branded search, 6,825 to 23,838 clicks (+249.3%), as Google started ranking the new product pages again for the head terms from about 17 October: "top up ml" 77 to 4,143 clicks (position 20.6 to 7.8), "top up ff" 551 to 2,406 (10.4 to 7.8) and "top up ml murah" 43 to 1,342 (14.9 to 6.9). The PDP page cut grew 172.2% (10,087 to 27,461) and the blog 113.2% (484 to 1,032).

Not fully recovered yet. In the last week before the move (21 to 27 September), non-branded search brought 52,293 clicks, "top up ff" ranked at 5.4 with 8,779 clicks and "top up roblox" at 3.6 with 2,860 clicks. This week non-branded is at 23,838, under half that level, "top up ff" still ranks lower (7.8), and "top up roblox" has not come back yet (only a few hundred impressions a week). Recovery after a migration takes time; this week is the first clear step in the right direction.

Read the position figure with care. The Indonesian average position looks worse (2.88 to 4.43), but only because non-branded queries, which rank lower than brand searches, came back into the mix. It is not a ranking loss.

Free Fire: two of our pages compete for "top up ff"

For Indonesian searches of "top up ff" this week, Google showed the Free Fire MAX PDP far more often (42,001 impressions, position 8.0, 1,516 clicks) than the Free Fire PDP (9,474 impressions, position 6.9, 875 clicks), which is the page meant for this search. Suggested action: make the Free Fire PDP the clear match for "top up ff" (title and main heading lead with Free Fire top up and Diamond), keep the Free Fire MAX PDP focused on "ff max" searches, and link from the Free Fire MAX PDP to the Free Fire PDP.

Brand searches: Google is moving them from the root gogogo.com to the new homepage

Since early October Google has shown the root gogogo.com to many Indonesian brand searchers, and it forwards them to gogogo.com/id-id. This week the new homepage doubled (15,057 to 29,889 clicks) while the root fell (34,140 to 18,387 Indonesian clicks), so together they held steady (49,197 to 48,276). The old register page, which took 33,090 clicks in the week of 28 September, now takes only a small share (297 clicks last week, 1,219 this week). Most brand searchers now land on the homepage, which is where they should land.

One to watch: the Poppo Live PDP moved up from position 6.3 to 3.0 and its impressions nearly doubled (23,712 to 44,409), yet clicks stayed flat (772 to 733). A better position without more clicks points to the title and snippet, not the ranking.
