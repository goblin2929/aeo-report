Executive Summary

Period in Review: lower totals against a one day surge, steady growth underneath

Combined clicks were 95,097 (-22.10% vs the prior week). The comparison week had one exceptional day:

  • Thursday 23 October surge. Brand searches jumped in Indonesia and the Philippines on the same day, together with Mobile Legends searches in Indonesia (Indonesia 27,418 clicks that day, the Philippines 25,272, against 8,321 and 1,719 the day before). Most of last week's growth came from that day.
  • Without that day, both markets grew. Indonesia averaged 10,493 clicks a day this week against 8,987 (+16.8%), the Philippines 3,003 against 2,479 (+21.1%).
  • Indonesian non-branded search keeps recovering: 23,838 to 27,668 clicks (+16.1%), about half of the move week (52,293 clicks, 21 to 27 September). Google now shows the Free Fire PDP for "top up ff" (clicks 875 to 3,133 for that search on the page).
  • Watch: "top up roblox" has not come back and now shows a new coins page instead of the Roblox PDP; the Philippine Mobile Legends First Recharge PDP slipped.
  • Malaysia +4.7%, impressions +33.0%.

How to read this

Average Google position (lower is better) for each market's champion product pages and their main searches (Indonesia: Mobile Legends, Free Fire, Free Fire MAX and Roblox; the Philippines: Mobile Legends, Mobile Legends First Recharge and Poppo Live), plus all Indonesian blog pages together (the blog runs in Indonesia only), week by week since each move, against September before the move (Indonesia 1 to 20 September, moved 21 September; the Philippines 1 to 27 September, moved 28 September). Searchers from the market's own country only, old and new address of each page combined. Under each position: clicks and impressions for that week (for September, the average per week, so it compares directly with the weekly columns). Change vs before the move = September position minus the latest week (positive, green = better than before the move). The search rows are the clearest recovery signal. A product page's average mixes every search that lands on it, so read it together with its clicks. Example: the Free Fire page's average position (5.3) is worse than in September (4.7), partly because "top up ff" has come back on it with a large share of the page's impressions at position 6.4, and partly because that search is still 1.2 positions below September (5.2). The 19 to 25 October column includes the 23 October surge, so its clicks are higher than a normal week.

Indonesia -9.7%: last week had a one day surge; leaving it out, clicks grew 16.8%

Counting gogogo.id, gogogo.com/id-id and Indonesian searchers on the root gogogo.com together, clicks went from 81,339 to 73,452. The drop comes from one day last week: on Thursday 23 October Indonesian clicks jumped to 27,418 (8,321 the day before), from brand searches ("gogogo", "gogogo id") and Mobile Legends searches ("top up ml" took 2,318 clicks that day against 217 the day before). Philippine searches surged on the same day. Leaving that day out, last week averaged 8,987 clicks a day and this week 10,493 a day (+16.8%). Search Console shows the surge but not what caused it.

Non-branded search kept growing: 23,838 to 27,668 clicks (+16.1%), led by "top up ff" (2,406 to 4,062 clicks, position 7.8 to 6.9) and "top up ff murah" (327 to 631, position 7.2 to 6.1). Brand clicks fell 20.4% (57,501 to 45,784) because the surge was mostly brand searches. Gogogo is still the first result for "gogogo" on every day of both weeks, so this is not a ranking loss. The query table shows "gogogo" at position 3.2 because that figure averages every Gogogo page Google lists for the search (homepage, root, login, register and more). The blog grew 54.9% (1,032 to 1,599) and PDP pages grew 2.4% (27,461 to 28,124).

Not fully recovered yet. Non-branded search is at about half the 52,293 clicks of the move week (21 to 27 September), when the old gogogo.id pages still ranked. "top up ml" eased slightly on its PDP (position 7.7 to 8.2, clicks flat at 3,881 while impressions grew to 100,853), and "top up roblox" has still not come back (see the Roblox note below).

Read the position figure with care. The Indonesian average position looks worse (4.43 to 5.32) because non-branded impressions, which rank lower than brand searches, grew from 408,324 to 601,138 while the brand surge fell away. It is not a ranking loss.

Free Fire: Google now shows the right page for "top up ff"

Last week Google showed the Free Fire MAX PDP far more often than the Free Fire PDP for Indonesian searches of "top up ff". This week it turned around: the Free Fire PDP, the page meant for this search, had 49,078 impressions (9,474 last week) at position 6.4 with 3,133 clicks (875 last week), and the Free Fire MAX PDP dropped to 24,861 impressions at position 7.9. Free Fire PDP clicks grew 69.6% (5,270 to 8,940). Related searches followed: "topup ff" (position 8.5 to 5.2 over 28 days), "top up ff murah" (7.2 to 5.9) and "top up ff termurah" (6.8 to 5.9) moved from far to close to target.

Roblox: Google shows the new coins page, not the Roblox PDP

"top up roblox" is still not back. This week Google showed the /id-id/coins page for it (667 impressions, position 9.6, 66 clicks), and the Roblox PDP got no impressions for this search (the older /id-id/p/topup/roblox-games page had 34 at position 6.9). The coins page is new in Search Console this week (7 to 13,516 impressions, 237 clicks) and also ranks for "topup robux murah" and "tempat top up roblox". Suggested action: link from the coins page to the Roblox PDP with the anchor "top up Roblox", so Google sees the Roblox PDP as the page for Roblox top up searches.

Brand searches: the homepage holds

The new homepage held steady (29,889 to 28,417 clicks, position 2.3 to 2.0), and Indonesian clicks on the root gogogo.com eased (18,387 to 13,514) as the 23 October surge fell away. The old register page dropped further (1,219 to 407 clicks), so brand searchers land on the homepage.
