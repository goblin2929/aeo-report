Executive Summary

Period in Review: topline eased on brand demand, but the transactional PDPs grew everywhere

Combined clicks eased to 99,567 (-3.99% vs the prior week) while impressions rose +14.54%. The dip is brand search softening in Indonesia, not our SEO pages, which grew:

  • Indonesia -11.5%, driven by branded search (-19.2%) and blog (-16.6%) demand fluctuation. Crucially the transactional PDPs grew +7.5% (Free Fire +1,096 clicks, Mobile Legends +878), so our money pages are healthy.
  • Philippines +19.3%, broad and genuine: PDP +39.9% (Mobile Legends +746), non-branded +32.0%, branded +17.8%.
  • Malaysia -7.4%, the usual demand trend shape: brand -10.2% but non-branded +9.5% and PDP +16.4%.
  • PDPs grew in all three markets (ID +7.5%, PH +39.9%, MY +16.4%), the clearest read that the search driven pages are working while brand demand cycles.
  • Broken page for the dev team: the ID PUBG Mobile UC PDP lost 89 clicks and all its indexing (impressions 1,191 to zero). A sibling URL returns a live 404, so this is a technical break to fix, not a ranking loss.

Why Indonesia eased 11.5%: softer brand demand, but the transactional PDPs grew

Indonesia eased 11.5% (77,127 to 68,231), driven by branded search softening: branded clicks fell 19.2% (35,029 to 28,294) and the blog eased 16.6%. This is demand fluctuation on the "gogogo" navigational terms, not a ranking problem; the brand position actually held (1.70 to 1.65).

The money pages moved the other way. The transactional PDPs grew +7.5% (23,994 to 25,800), led by Free Fire (+1,096 clicks) and Mobile Legends (+878), with Free Fire Max adding +175. Non-brand eased only 5.1%. Our SEO driven pages are healthy; the topline dip is brand demand.

Broken page for the dev team (not a ranking slip): the PUBG Mobile UC PDP lost 89 clicks with position collapsing 10.8 to 15.1 and impressions crashing 1,191 to 286, then to zero on 17 and 18 August. The URL /p/topup/uc-pubgm-indonesia returns a live 404, and the ranked page /p/topup/uc-pubgm-indonesia-games lost all its indexing, the signature of a page that returned errors during the week. This is a technical break to fix (confirm the canonical URL, 301 the 404 to the live page, restore indexing), not an SEO ranking problem. By contrast the Roblox voucher page lost 135 clicks while its rank held (2.2 to 2.0), an ordinary demand dip.
