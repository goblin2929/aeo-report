Why AI visibility fell: cited more often, named less often Visibility counts the answers that name ExpertCoach.Co in the text, on 30 fixed questions. The drop is on Google’s two assistants, and it is not a loss of sources:
  • Across real searches, AI exposure grew. Comparing 1 to 21 October with 1 to 21 November, Google showed expertcoach.co in its AI features 6,023 times against 8,683 (+44%), and Bing’s AI answers cited expertcoach.co 1,893 times against 2,816 (+49%).
  • The number of answers did not change: each assistant answered the same 30 questions about 28 to 30 times a day in both months.
  • Google cites fewer sources per answer (AI Overview 8.6 to 8.0, Gemini 8.3 to 7.0), yet ExpertCoach.Co pages are cited in more answers: AI Overview 46% to 49%, Gemini 49% to 69%.
  • What fell is the name in the text. Answers that use an ExpertCoach.Co page as a source without naming ExpertCoach.Co rose from 142 to 375 across the two. About three in ten are questions that explain the exam, such as “How hard is the ICF ACC exam, and how long should I study for it?” and “What does the ICF PCC certification exam actually test?”, where the answer explains the exam from our pages and recommends no provider. Most of the rest ask how to study or what to look for in a course, where the answer cites our pages but names no provider.
  • On Gemini this is market wide: answers naming any of the brands we track (the ICF left out) fell from 64% to 42%, and Coaching Outside the Box (30% to 20%) and CoachCertify (15% to 10%) fell with us. On AI Overview it is not: brands overall held (56% to 54%) while CredentialPrep (6% to 10%) and CoachCertify (4% to 9%) gained.
  • Still the leader on Google: after the ICF, ExpertCoach.Co is the most named brand on AI Overview (33%, next Coaching Outside the Box 16%) and on Gemini (33%, next Coaching Outside the Box 20%), and expertcoach.co is the most cited exam prep site on both (445 and 814 citations). On ChatGPT, CredentialPrep moved ahead: named in 26% of answers against 20% for ExpertCoach.Co, and credentialprep.com cited 683 times against 404 for expertcoach.co.

Method

Search Console for expertcoach.co (every language version). The two halves are pulled as two exact date ranges; the weekly charts are pulled week by week so no week is cut short by a row limit. Brand queries (ExpertCoach and its spellings) are removed before anything is counted (101 brand clicks and 408 impressions in the first 26 days, 183 and 557 in the last 26). The 30 prompts ExpertCoach.Co tracks in WorkDuo also appear in Search Console, because the tracking runs them through Google; they are our own tracking, not demand, and are removed (30 queries, 512 impressions, no clicks). No query met the automated query rule used for other sites (no click in the window, at least 3,000 impressions, 80% or more in its three busiest weeks). Each query is tested against the subtopic rows in order and the first match wins, so the specific always beats the general: comparison and markers first, then paid prep wording (prep, course, simulator, how to pass, study), then free practice wording (practice, sample, mock, questions), then exam facts, then credential facts. Position is weighted by impressions. Refreshed and new tags come from the Content Delivery Tracker (Month 1 and Month 2, English pages). Search Console withholds rare queries, so per query totals sit below the site totals in the interface; both halves are withheld the same way, so the comparison holds. AI search: WorkDuo Data API daily metrics and Core API citations (own URLs, deduplicated for scheme, www and trailing slash), 18 September to 23 November 2026; every WorkDuo answer for 1 to 23 October and 1 to 23 November for the cause of the visibility change. Search Console generative AI features report and Bing Webmaster Tools AI performance report, both exported on 24 November 2026. Google Analytics visits from AI assistants, paid ads excluded.
