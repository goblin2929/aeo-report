Methodology & Validation

Adult and piracy query exclusion. From 22 August a spam network pointed adult and piracy anchor text at 22 Decube pages, and Google Indonesia began returning the Decube homepage for those queries. In raw Search Console data this shows as 2,918 August clicks against 2,161 in July, which would read as growth. It is not. Every figure in this report removes it, in two layers. Layer one: a 112 term regex covering the bokep family and its misspellings, explicit adult vocabulary in English and Bahasa Indonesia, adult streaming and piracy hosts, and the strings Search Console masks with asterisks. The pattern was built from the complete 25,576 row August query export and audited in both directions, so every match was reviewed for false positives and every non-match reviewed for leakage. It removes 1,194 of August's clicks and 99 of July's, and a page-dimension check confirms 100% of them landed on the homepage. Layer two: Indonesia and India are excluded from every period in the charts and the segment tables. Search Console anonymises rare queries, so a long tail of adult clicks is never labelled and the regex cannot see it; Indonesian clicks went from a 53 to 125 per month baseline to 1,473 in August, and Indian clicks were inflated to 1,182 in June and 959 in July by the same network's South Asian arm. Excluding both markets uniformly across all eight months is the only way to compare months honestly. For reference, on the adult-regex-only basis August totals 1,724 clicks against July's 2,062.

Dimension discipline. Site totals and the charts use the date dimension, which never double counts. Page and query breakdowns use their own dimensions and are labelled as such. Page-dimension impressions are counted once per page shown and so run above the date-dimension totals; page-dimension clicks cross-validate at 1.007 against date-dimension clicks for August and 1.003 for July. A caution recorded for future reports: combining a country filter with the page dimension makes Search Console suppress roughly 44% of clicks, so all page-level tables here are pulled unfiltered and corrected by subtraction instead.

AEO measurement. Visibility is the share of tracked AI responses in which Decube is mentioned, averaged across the days in the month. A response is one engine asked one prompt on one day. WorkDuo re-asks the panel daily; August carries 31 days of data and July carries 27 (tracking started 5 July). The prompt panel is identical across both months, so no part of the gain comes from adding prompts. Google AI Overview and ChatGPT are read with the singular platform= filter, verified against the cross-platform aggregate so the filter is known to apply.

AI sessions come from GA4 directly, not from a dashboard estimate: sessions whose sessionSource matches the AI platform list (chatgpt, openai, perplexity, gemini, bard, claude, anthropic, copilot, you.com, poe, deepseek, grok, mistral, phind, searchgpt, edgeservices, aimode). The same definition is used for every client and every month.
