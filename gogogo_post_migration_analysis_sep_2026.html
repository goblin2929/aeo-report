How this is measured

On 21 September the Indonesian site moved from gogogo.id to gogogo.com/id-id. During a domain migration Google keeps showing the old URLs in search for a while, then swaps to the new domain page by page as it reindexes, so traffic splits across both properties. To read the true Indonesian condition we combine both: gogogo.id (old) plus the gogogo.com /id-id section (new).

The new domain figures use the gogogo.com domain property filtered to the /id-id section, which correctly includes the /id-id homepage (a URL prefix property would miss it). Latest fully settled day is 26 September; the last day or two can still rise slightly.

1. Combined traffic condition: is there a decline?

No decline. Combined Indonesian clicks grew +15.3% across the migration.

Important caveat: it is early

The migration is only six days old and Google is still reassigning URLs page by page. Migrations often show a delayed dip in the following two to four weeks before recovering. The site wide picture is healthy today, but this needs weekly monitoring until the new domain fully replaces the old one in the index.

2. PDP condition and signs of recovery: Free Fire, Mobile Legends, Roblox

The three key PDPs are entering the URL handover, and the new pages are already being indexed and ranked

Through about 24 September the combined clicks for all three PDPs tracked their pre migration levels. Around 25 to 26 September Google began swapping the indexed URL from gogogo.id to gogogo.com/id-id for Free Fire and Mobile Legends, which is the normal page by page handover. The new gogogo.com/id-id pages are climbing day by day at healthy positions, which is the recovery signal, but they have not yet reached the old click volume, so those two show a short transition dip in the last day or two. Roblox has not started its handover yet, so it is still at full strength on the old URL.

Verdict: early signs of recovery are clearly present (the new URLs are indexed and rising with good positions), but Free Fire and Mobile Legends are mid handover and have not yet reached parity, so it is too early to call full recovery on them. Monitor the next one to two weeks for the new URLs to catch up.

Summary

  • No traffic loss so far. Combined Indonesian clicks are +15.3% across the migration, and demand kept rising through it.
  • The new domain is being indexed and is ramping (8 to 3,657 clicks per day in six days), which is the healthy signal that the migration is transferring.
  • Free Fire and Mobile Legends are mid handover with a short transition dip on the newest days while the new URLs climb; Roblox has not handed over yet and is still at full strength.
  • It is early. Six days in, the picture is healthy, but migrations can dip in weeks two to four. We will monitor the combined figure and the three PDPs weekly until the new domain fully replaces the old in the index.
