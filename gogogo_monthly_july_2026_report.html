Executive Summary

A strong month: all three markets grew

Combined clicks rose to 393,187 (+12.17% vs June, well above the 3.3% extra-day effect). Every market grew, with two clear engines:

  • Philippines was the standout, +39.61%. This was a genuine step up in both volume and quality: branded clicks +44.09%, the homepage position improved from 3.05 to 2.35, and the Mobile Legends PDP from 2.08 to 2.01. Non-branded also grew +13.21%.
  • Indonesia +5.11%, led by Mobile Legends product pages. The ID PDP segment grew +11.14% on +18.91% impressions, driven by the Mobile Legends PDP (+58.07% clicks, +81.73% impressions to about 2.17 million). This includes the late-July Mobile Legends event, and it makes the ML PDP the single largest opportunity on the whole site (about 2.17M impressions at position 4.83). Free Fire cooled the other way (-23.03%).
  • Malaysia +38.13%, broad growth on a small base: non-branded +62.71%, PDP +55.65%, and positions improving (PDP 4.72 to 4.38).
  • Indonesia blog eased -25.33%, but its positions held roughly steady (segment 5.46 to 5.56, and the top page spasi-kosong-nama-free-fire even improved slightly, 5.95 to 5.81). Clicks and impressions down while position holds is a seasonal or trend decline in demand, not a content problem: we are noting it and monitoring, not treating it as an urgent fix.
  • Branded strength everywhere: ID "gogogo id" +60.81%, PH "gogogo" +62.24%, MY "gogogo" +48.82%. The brand is compounding.

The Mobile Legends story at month level

Mobile Legends drove Indonesia's product growth this month, amplified by a late-July event (peak Tuesday 21 July). The pattern is captured demand, with the ranking roughly holding:
