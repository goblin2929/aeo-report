Everything the Import File Needs

Five jobs that usually take five systems. Here is how each one runs on a single shipment record.

ISF Filing Software

File ISF-10 and ISF-5 Without Leaving the Shipment

The ISF pulls from the booking and the house bill already on the file, so your team is not rekeying importer, consignee, and container data into a separate portal. Both ISF-10 and ISF-5 are supported in product through integrated filing partners, and the filing status lands back on the shipment where operations can see it.

  • ISF data sourced from the shipment record, not a spreadsheet
  • Filing response visible on the file, not in a separate inbox
  • Smart To-Do Lists flag the files where the ISF is still open

AMS Filing Software

Ocean AMS Filing Tied to the House Bill You Issued

Transmit ocean AMS through an integrated filing partner from inside GoFreight. The manifest references the same house bill the platform generated, so the filed data and the issued document never drift apart. Carrier and terminal movements flow in through the CargoSmart integration, so the file stays current after the manifest goes out.

  • Ocean AMS submitted against the house bill on the file
  • EDI sets 300, 301, 304, 315, and 997 supported in EDIFACT, X.12, XML, and JSON
  • AES for US ocean and air exports through AESDirect on the export side

HBL Software

Generate HBL and MBL PDFs From the Shipment Record

GoFreight produces both house and master bill PDFs from data already on the file, so the bill your customer receives matches the manifest you filed. Memos attach to the HBL, the MBL, the container, or the trade partner, and stay visible from the HBL list, the MBL list, and the container list in one place.

  • House and master bill PDFs generated in platform
  • Memo system across HBL, MBL, container, and trade partner
  • One HBL list view for the whole desk, not one folder per operator

Ocean Import Documentation

Arrival Notice, Delivery Order, and the Rest of the Import Pack

Arrival notice, delivery and pickup order, commercial invoice, packing list, and booking documents all generate from the same shipment. Smart To-Do Lists trigger by shipment type and customer condition, so the arrival notice goes out when the MBL status updates rather than when somebody remembers. Tracking comes from ocean carriers, railroads, terminals, and AIS, and the customer portal gives unlimited tracking users their own view.

  • Arrival notice, delivery order, commercial invoice, packing list, booking
  • Smart To-Do Lists trigger by shipment type and customer condition
  • Up to 53% reduction in demurrage and detention fees

Freight Forwarding Accounting with QuickBooks

Every Charge Tied to Its File, Synced to QuickBooks Online

Charges carry from quote to shipment to invoice automatically, so nothing is retyped between operations and accounting. GoFreight uses shipment based accounting, which means every charge stays attached to the file that created it and margin per file is visible before month end. The native QuickBooks Online integration closes the loop, and multi entity operations get separate GLs, billing codes, currencies, and bank configurations.

  • Native QuickBooks Online integration
  • Shipment based accounting, margin visible per file
  • Overdue AR, over credit limit, and customer ranking reports built in
