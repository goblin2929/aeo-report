Gogogo ID Blog Performance

1 January to 20 September 2026. Daily and weekly search performance, split into the pages NovaStacks created or refreshed and the pages NovaStacks has never touched.
Source: Google Search Console, property https://gogogo.id/, web search, pages containing /blog/. Content attribution source: the Flip content planner sheet, Gogogo tab, read live on 23 September 2026. Data pulled 23 September 2026. Latest day available in Search Console: 20 September 2026.
1. How to read this report

Definitions used everywhere below

Blog
Every gogogo.id URL containing /blog/. 1,385 distinct blog URLs received at least one impression in the window.
New
An article NovaStacks wrote and Gogogo published at a URL that did not exist before. 102 rows are live in the content planner, 94 of them have Search Console data.
Refreshed
An existing article NovaStacks rewrote. 20 rows are live in the content planner, 19 of them have Search Console data.
Never touched
Every other blog URL: the legacy library that existed before the engagement and that NovaStacks has not edited. 1,272 URLs. It also holds all traffic earned before 29 July 2026 by the pages we later created or refreshed, for the reason in the next row.
The cutoff
29 July 2026. That is the first day any NovaStacks page recorded an impression in Search Console, so it is the honest boundary between before and after. The contractual start is the July content month.
How a page is credited
A page counts as New or Refreshed only from 29 July 2026 onward. Anything it earned before that date is credited to never touched, because on those days NovaStacks had not touched it yet. That is why the New and Refreshed columns are zero in every week up to 26 July and first carry a value in the week of 27 July to 2 August. Crediting a refreshed page's old traffic to us would overstate our contribution and understate the legacy library.
Weekly buckets
Monday to Sunday. 37 complete weeks, 5 January to 20 September 2026. The 4 days from 1 to 4 January fall in an incomplete week and appear only in the daily charts and the monthly table.

Read the two comparisons together, not one of them alone

Against the whole January to July baseline the blog is up sharply. Against the 54 days immediately before our content went live it is down. Both are true, and the reason is that the legacy blog peaked in June, before NovaStacks content existed. The rest of this report separates the two stories: what the legacy library is doing on its own, and what our pages are adding on top.

The three findings this data supports

  • NovaStacks pages are the only growing part of the blog. They went from 0 clicks before 29 July to 2,609 clicks in the week of 14 to 20 September, which is 24.0% of all blog clicks. Every other segment is flat or falling.
  • The legacy library peaked in June and is declining on demand, not on ranking. Never touched impressions fell from 2,817,184 in June to 2,192,884 in August while their CTR held between 2.21% and 2.24%. Positions and click rate are steady, the searches themselves have thinned out after the Indonesian school holidays.
  • The blog is taking a bigger share of the site. Blog clicks are 12.3% of all gogogo.id clicks since 29 July against 6.9% across January to July, because site wide clicks grew only +5.1% per day while blog clicks grew +87.1% per day over the same baseline.

8. Why the never touched pages are falling

Demand, not ranking

The never touched library lost 21.7% of its clicks between the two matched 54 day windows. That is a demand movement, not a ranking loss, and the CTR column is the proof: never touched CTR ran 2.22% in June, 2.21% in July, 2.24% in August and 1.96% in the first 20 days of September, while impressions fell from 2,817,184 in June to 1,248,058 across 20 days of September. When positions slip, CTR falls first. Here CTR held and impressions moved, which means the searches thinned out.

The June peak was broad, not a single viral page. The ten largest May to June gains were all existing Free Fire and Mobile Legends nickname, cheat and sensitivity pages, which is the standard Indonesian school holiday pattern for gaming content. Those same pages are now back near their pre holiday level.

10. Data notes and limits
Attribution source
Which pages count as ours comes only from the Flip content planner sheet, Gogogo tab, and only rows whose URL Production cell is filled. A row with no production URL is treated as not live and is excluded.
Rows not counted
Of 122 live rows, 8 valid gogogo.id URLs have no Search Console data yet, so they carry no clicks or impressions here: /blog/games/free-fire/jadwal-mystery-shop-free-fire, /blog/games/free-fire/tips-bermain-solo-ranked-ff, /blog/games/hero-terkuat-di-mobile-legends, /blog/games/alpha-ml-mobile-legends, /blog/games/coa-ml-mobile-legends, /blog/games/cara-hapus-akun-ml-mobile-legends, /blog/games/nama-ff-rumit and /blog/games/inspirasi-simbol-cina-ff-keren. One further row, an August new article, has a Google Docs draft link in the URL Production column instead of a live URL, so it cannot be matched to Search Console at all.
Refresh dates are not tracked
The planner records a contractual month, not the date a page went live or was rewritten. Because of that, 29 July 2026 is used as the single credit date for every NovaStacks page rather than a per page go live date, which is the conservative choice: a page refreshed in September is still credited from 29 July, so its August performance counts as ours even though we had not rewritten it yet. 16 of the 19 refreshed pages first appear in Search Console between 28 July and 8 August 2026, which points to a URL change at rewrite rather than an edit in place.
Five pages predate the engagement
Three refreshed pages carry impressions from 1 January 2026 (/blog/games/cara-main-roblox-di-laptop, /blog/games/daftar-karakter-free-fire and /blog/games/daftar-skin-zodiac-mobile-legends), and two pages labelled New already existed: /blog/games/nama-ff-elite-cees from 8 February 2026 and /blog/games/kenapa-ff-tidak-bisa-login from 26 April 2026. Everything those five earned before 29 July 2026 is credited to never touched, not to us. The First Impression column in section 7 shows the true first date for every page, so a date earlier than 29 July there means the URL predates our work on it.
September is partial
20 of 30 days. Every September figure in this report is the 20 day actual unless it is explicitly labelled as a 30 day pace.
Search Console lag
Roughly 3 days. 20 September 2026 is the latest complete day at the time of the pull.
Market scope
Indonesia only. gogogo.game/en-ph and gogogo.com/en-my have no blog, so there is nothing to compare there.
