FAQ

Questions about the Decube AI analytics platform

What is AI analytics?

AI analytics lets business teams ask questions about company data in plain language and get answers from the data itself, without writing SQL or waiting for a report. Decube AI Analytics answers from metrics your team has approved, so everyone works from the same numbers.

Do we need a semantic layer before we start?

No. Decube builds the semantic layer from your warehouse, ETL and BI layer. Business teams describe a metric in plain language, Decube AI drafts the definition, and the data team approves it once for every answer.

How does Decube AI Analytics keep answers consistent?

Every metric has one approved definition with an owner. Every answer in Decube, and in the AI assistants connected through MCP, uses that definition, so the same question gets the same number.

Can we ask questions in ChatGPT, Claude or Copilot?

Yes, through MCP. Your AI assistant uses Decube's approved metrics, so its answers follow the same definitions. Availability depends on the connector and on your assistant's MCP support.

How is this different from connecting ChatGPT directly to our warehouse?

An assistant connected straight to a warehouse has to guess what each metric means from table and column names, so two people can get two different numbers. Decube gives the assistant one approved definition for each metric.

How is Decube AI Analytics different from TrustyAI?

TrustyAI reads metadata: it finds and explains data assets in your catalog. Decube AI Analytics answers business questions from the data itself, on approved metrics.

How long does setup take?

Onboarding is tailored and takes about 2 to 3 hours, with test datasets for marketing and finance teams.
