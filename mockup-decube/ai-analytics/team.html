Who is "your team"?

The page says "your team" several times without saying who that is. Decube AI Analytics has three teams, and each line on the page should name the one it means.

The three teams behind the product

TeamWho they areWhat they do in Decube AI Analytics
Business team
buyer and main user		Senior business leaders: VP or Head of Marketing and sales leaders first, plus finance and operations leaders, at mid market companies of about 500 to 600 employeesAsk questions about revenue, margin and pipeline in plain English and decide what to do, without waiting for the data team to pull a report. Describe a new metric in their own words.
Data team
approver		The company's small data team, about 3 to 4 peopleConnect the warehouse, ETL and BI layer, check each metric's calculation, dimensions and owner in code (YAML), and approve it once so every answer uses it.
Metric ownerThe business function that owns what a metric means, for example Finance for net revenueStands behind the definition of their metric.
Decube AI Analytics frees business leaders from waiting on their data team for reports. The business team comes in first and asks; the data team joins to approve the definitions those answers use.
