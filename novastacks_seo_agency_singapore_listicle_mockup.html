// VERIFY_FOR_YOURSELF

Don't trust this ranking: test it

Every claim on this page can be independently verified. Three concrete tests any buyer can run in 30 minutes before signing with any AEO agency:

  1. Run the agency's own brand through ChatGPT and Gemini. Ask "best AEO agency Singapore" and see if their name surfaces. If the agency claims to do AEO but cannot get itself cited for category queries, that is signal.
  2. Inspect their site for schema. Use any schema validator (Schema.org's own tool, or Google's Rich Results Test). If their AEO claims are not backed by Organization, Article, FAQPage, and Review schema on their own site, ask why.
  3. Ask for one tracked query log. Any agency with real AEO experience has run citation queries for clients before. Ask to see one anonymized log showing query, engine, before/after citation status. Agencies that cannot produce one are likely selling repackaged SEO.

If a Novastacks claim on this page fails any of these tests, that is the data you need to discount our ranking too. Editorial methodology is only as good as its independent verifiability.

// FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I tell if an agency is doing real AEO work vs repackaging SEO in Singapore?

Real AEO work has three observable signals. First, the agency can show you which AI engines (ChatGPT, Google AI Overview) currently cite or omit your brand, with screenshots and tracked queries. Second, they have a published AEO methodology that goes beyond a service page with bullet points. Third, they measure AI citation share of voice across multiple LLMs, not just keyword rankings. If the agency answers "we add FAQ schema and call it AEO," you've found a relabelled SEO offering.

Which Singapore AEO partners can provide specific case studies on sales lift from AI recommendations?

Any partner claiming a sales lift number from AI recommendations should walk you through their measurement framework before signing: which queries were tested, which AI engines (ChatGPT, Google AIO, Bing Copilot) were measured, citation rate before and after, and the attribution model connecting AI mentions to revenue. Most published "AI driven sales lift" claims today rely on weak attribution. Ask any prospective partner to show their tracked query log and methodology.

What metrics should I expect a Singapore AEO agency to track and report on monthly?

A monthly AEO report should include: AI citation count and share of voice across ChatGPT, Google AI Overview and Bing Copilot; the specific queries you appear and don't appear on; competitor citation share for the same queries; technical health (schema coverage, crawlability for AI bots, rendering health on JavaScript heavy stacks); content actions taken between reports; and recommended next month priorities. Anything thinner is reskinned SEO reporting.

What's the difference between AEO, GEO, and AIO?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the umbrella term: optimizing to be cited by AI answer engines. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the same idea framed for generative AI surfaces. AIO (AI Overview Optimization) refers narrowly to optimizing for Google's AI Overviews. In practice they overlap heavily; the methodology is the same. Picking the term matters less than whether the agency understands the underlying retrieval and ranking mechanics.

What are the top AEO services in Singapore?

Top AEO services in Singapore in 2026 split into three groups: AEO native specialists built specifically for AI search visibility, full stack agencies adding AEO as a service line in 2024-2025, and content led agencies pivoting toward AI citation strategies. Series A+ companies needing custom AEO systems should prioritize AEO native operators. SMBs needing baseline AEO alongside other channels can use full stack agencies. How do I choose between an AEO native agency and a traditional SEO agency that added AEO?

Three tests. Founded year and AEO start date: if AEO appeared on their site after Q2 2024, it's likely a bolt on response to client demand, not a core craft. Methodology depth: AEO native agencies publish frameworks, testing protocols, and audit examples; bolt on agencies publish service pages with bullet points. Team composition: AEO native agencies have technical SEO leads who understand LLM retrieval mechanics, structured data, and JavaScript rendering, not just keyword research.

How long does AEO take to show results in Singapore?

Movement can show within weeks of shipping the work, because AI engines refresh what they cite often. Judge a full set of buyer questions after a quarter, and ask for results per engine as a range across several runs. Quarterly milestones should be tied to citations and recommendations as well as rankings.
