What happened to Globe: position reached its best weekly level, clicks near flat

The aggregate Globe position improved from 7.58 to 7.14, its best weekly level since we took charge and better than the 7.31 of the week ending 13 September. Clicks were near flat (303 to 294, -3.0%) on a small base.

GSC caveat: most Globe impressions are anonymized, so the position is a page level figure; the improving position is the reliable read this week.

Globe position trend since July: we are in a better position now

On a monthly average basis the Flip Globe position has improved over the period we have managed it: 8.47 in July, 10.12 in August (the dip we diagnosed as the anonymized long tail broadening, not a ranking loss), and 7.44 so far in September (1 to 27). September is the strongest of the three and better than July, so the direction is clearly positive. Position is a monthly average, so it compares fairly even though September is a partial month.
