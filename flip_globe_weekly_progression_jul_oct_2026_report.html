Summary

This page brings the weekly reports together into one view: how each area and each target keyword moved week by week, from the first reported week (6 to 12 July) to the last (28 September to 4 October). To keep single week noise out of the conclusions, the change column compares the average of the first 4 weeks (6 July to 2 August) with the average of the last 4 weeks (7 September to 4 October).

What moved

  • Flip Globe position improved from 8.71 to 7.22, and the last week (4 Oct) was the best of the 13 at 6.79. Weekly clicks grew 12.0% (296 to 332 a week) while impressions fell 6.6%, so the gain came from ranking better, not from more demand.
  • Non branded Globe searches improved from 7.85 to 6.63. The two main Globe keywords moved into the Top 3: "transfer uang ke luar negeri" 4.5 to 2.3, and "kirim uang ke luar negeri" 4.9 to 2.2. China and Thailand also moved to the top positions, on small volumes (about 5 to 30 Indonesian impressions a week).
  • Globe related blogs improved from 7.49 to 7.00, with clicks up 3.9% a week. Clicks from the blogs Novastacks produced or refreshed grew from 49 to 889 a week, reaching 1,310 in the last week.
  • Currency Converter held its position, but none of its 20 target keywords held the target. Indonesian converter clicks fell 18.6% with impressions down 7.8% (lower demand) at a steady position (6.88 to 6.98). Several Indonesian keywords slipped one to two positions ("yen ke rupiah" 5.5 to 7.7, "kurs ringgit ke rupiah" 6.9 to 8.8); the English side stayed flat. Title and Meta Description editing on the converter pages was still an open request during the period.
  • Malaysia went the other way: "kirim uang ke malaysia" eased from 2.9 to 5.5. The page ranking for it is the blog flip.id/id/blog/cara-transfer-uang-ke-malaysia, not the Malaysia Globe page.
