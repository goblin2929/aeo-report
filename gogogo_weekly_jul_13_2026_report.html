Executive Summary

Period in Review: a strong week, led by Indonesia

Combined clicks jumped to 125,017 (+67.47%), driven overwhelmingly by Indonesia (+89.32%). The ID surge is two things at once, and it is important to separate them:

  • A promotional spike (branded, likely temporary). A Seabank 5th anniversary and DANA e-wallet campaign drove branded clicks +229.20%. The fingerprints are everywhere: "gogogo seabank" (448 clicks, new), "gogogo dana" (+2,527%), the promo page /promo/Seabank5th (728 clicks, new), and the e-wallet pages /p/e-wallet/dana (64 to 942) and /e-wallet (7 to 845). This is real traffic, but campaign-driven branded demand typically normalises once the promo ends.
  • Durable SEO gains (the part that stays). The ID homepage improved from position 4.31 to 2.96 (clicks 23,429 to 63,618), and the two flagship PDPs gained rank with more impressions: Free Fire top up 4.77 to 4.27 (+47.80% impressions) and Mobile Legends top up 4.82 to 3.74 (+41.40%). Non-branded core also grew a healthy +14.47%, led by "top up ml" (+43.77%, position 6.16 to 4.54).
  • Philippines: steady, healthy growth (+8.27%). The homepage improved to position 2.71 and non-branded impressions grew +30.23% on Poppo Live recharge demand. Wesing and Honor of Kings still rank at position 1 with near-zero CTR, the standing title opportunity.
  • Malaysia: up on brand only, non-branded pulled back. Clicks rose +5.94% but entirely from branded (+9.33%); non-branded fell -11.98% and PDP -12.35% as last period's Free Fire top up breakout cooled. The homepage position also slipped (4.73 to 5.75). A soft week for MY discovery.
  • ID blog softened slightly (-6.94%), a normal give-back after prior gains; positions held.
