Each phone shows the same page in the mobile design, with one version of the text. Scores come from one blind review on 25 November in which all versions of a page were scored together, so compare them within a page (0 to 100, higher is better; a gap of 11 points or less is close to a tie); "flagged" counts sentences a parent could not easily restate (fewer is better). Each phone scrolls on its own.
