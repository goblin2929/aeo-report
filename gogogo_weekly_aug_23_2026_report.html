Executive Summary

Period in Review: Indonesia rebounded strongly as its rankings recovered

Combined clicks rose to 115,517 (+15.25% vs the prior week) on impressions up +16.23%. Indonesia drove all the gain, recovering the rankings it slipped on the prior week:

  • Indonesia +27.0%, a genuine rank led rebound: PDP +54.8% (Mobile Legends +8,374 clicks with rank improving 5.1 to 4.9, Free Fire +4,894 improving 4.9 to 4.6), non-branded +42% at a higher CTR. The head terms "top up ff" and "top up ml" climbed back up the page.
  • Philippines -10.6%, softer branded demand (brand -12.2%, and brand is about 87% of PH); the SEO pages held (PDP +5.1%, non-branded flat).
  • Malaysia -10.3%, a quiet demand week on a very small base; brand CTR actually improved as low intent cross-locale impressions receded.
  • Indonesia brand CTR recovered from 27.8% to 30.6%, partly reversing the prior week's brand click through dip.
  • Context: the competitor query "codashop" nearly doubled its impressions again (262k to 494k); it inflates Indonesia's non-branded impressions but is not real Gogogo demand.

Indonesia rebounded +27.0%: the money pages recovered their rankings

Indonesia jumped 27.0% (68,852 to 87,446), and it is a genuine, rank led recovery. The transactional PDPs grew +54.8% (25,876 to 40,048), led by Mobile Legends (+8,374 clicks, position improved 5.1 to 4.9) and Free Fire (+4,894, position improved 4.9 to 4.6).

The head non-branded terms reversed last week's slip. "top up ff" climbed from position 5.6 to 4.8 and clicks jumped 5,473 to 9,020; "top up ml" from 6.6 to 5.7 (2,304 to 4,899); "top up ml murah" 6.2 to 5.5; "top up roblox" 3.7 to 3.2. Non-branded clicks grew +42% (40,542 to 57,414) and its CTR rose (2.68% to 3.21%). This is the rankings we lost the prior week coming back.

Branded also recovered: clicks +6.1% (28,310 to 30,032) with the brand CTR lifting from 27.8% to 30.6%. The one distortion to keep in mind is "codashop" (a competitor's brand), whose impressions nearly doubled again (262k to 494k); it inflates non-branded impressions but is not real Gogogo demand.

Philippines eased 10.6% on softer brand demand; the SEO pages held

Philippines eased 10.6% (30,077 to 26,899), driven by branded demand softening: branded clicks fell 12.2% (26,575 to 23,332) and brand is about 87% of Philippine clicks, so it sets the topline. The brand CTR eased from 66.8% to 60.7%.

The SEO-facing pages held up: PDP grew +5.1% (3,018 to 3,173) with its CTR improving (1.62% to 1.84%), led by the Poppo Live PDP (+185 clicks), and non-branded clicks were flat to slightly up (3,502 to 3,567). The dip is brand search fluctuation, not our content.
