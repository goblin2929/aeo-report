Executive Summary

Period in Review

Across the three markets, combined clicks softened to 145,708 (-18.97%) against the prior 14 day window, driven mainly by Indonesia, the largest property. The decline is concentrated in branded search (brand name queries such as "gogogo" and "gogogo id"), which fluctuates with seasonal and campaign demand, while the 44 article content batch for ID is still in production and its impact will land in later periods. Key movements:

  • Indonesia (gogogo.id): Clicks 112,587 (-20.54%), impressions 2,492,009 (-14.86%). Branded clicks fell -29.74% and the two flagship product pages (Free Fire and Mobile Legends top up) drove most of the drop.
  • Philippines (gogogo.game/en-ph): Clicks 30,887 (-14.08%), impressions 87,210 (-7.09%). The market remains almost entirely branded, led by "gogogo ph" and Poppo Live recharge.
  • Malaysia (gogogo.com/en-my): The only market that grew. Clicks 2,234 (+1.92%) with Non-Branded clicks up +42.49%, led by newly ranking Free Fire top up queries.
  • Rising category, Roblox: In ID, "top up roblox" (+271.97%) and "top up robux" (+1,011.27%) surged even as Free Fire and Mobile Legends cooled, pointing to a shift in demand worth capturing with content and PDP focus.

Next Action Items: PDP Title Optimization
