What happened to Globe: the hub slipped in rank, but the Indonesian destination pages held

Globe clicks fell 17.6% and the average position slipped from 8.67 to 10.48. Splitting the pages shows the drop is not broad, it is two specific things:

  • The /id/globe hub is the real ranking slip: impression-weighted position 7.58 to 11.76 (down about 4 positions, into page two), losing 16 clicks. This is the one genuine rank loss to investigate.
  • The Indonesian destination pages (44) held their rank: impression-weighted position 6.13 to 6.18, essentially flat. The click drivers moved within a position (philippines 5.0 to 5.8, malaysia improved 5.7 to 4.2, thailand 5.6 to 6.4, china flat at 9.7). They lost 54 clicks combined, but with rank flat that is a demand dip, not a ranking loss.
  • The English destination pages (42) slid deeper into page two and three (14.41 to 17.03), reversing last week's climb, but they rank so low that clicks barely moved (+12). Volatile because they sit deep, not a priority.

GSC caveat: most Globe impressions are anonymized, so the aggregate 8.67 to 10.48 is a page-level figure; the split above is the reliable read. The action item is the /id/globe hub (technical check: indexing, internal links, any recent change to the hub) if it persists next week. The Indonesian destination pages, the click engine, are rank-stable.

One anomaly to note: /id/globe/malaysia improved its rank (5.7 to 4.2) and doubled impressions (1,601 to 3,307) yet lost 9 clicks. That is a CTR or SERP-feature issue specific to that page, not a ranking loss.

What happened to Converter: Indonesian mixed, English stuck at its page two ceiling

Converter Indonesian eased 4.9% on a mixed picture, not a broad decline. The Ringgit pair idr-mys-myr (-36 clicks) and the Korean Won krw-kor-idr (-23) lost clicks on softer demand, while the US Dollar usd-usa-idr (+27), the Japanese Yen jpy-jpn-idr (+24) and the Vietnamese Dong vnd-vnm-idr (+22) gained. Net demand was slightly down.

The Japanese Yen recovered from last week. The pair we flagged as slipping (the head query "1 yen berapa rupiah") bounced back this week: the page added 24 clicks as its position improved from 7.5 to 6.9. That is the kind of head query recovery a ranking push targets.

Converter English eased 14.1% on small absolute numbers. The head page usd-usa-idr remains stuck around position 10 (page two), still the largest untapped ceiling. Pulling the head English pairs (usd to idr, sgd to idr) onto page one is where the English converter's growth sits.
