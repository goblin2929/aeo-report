Paid Search Report: launch to 22 November

Google Ads, three non-brand campaigns, with the Brand test reported separately · GoFreight and Novastacks
23 November 2026 · Monday
Period: 16 Sep to 22 Nov 2026 (launch to the last full day) · Charts show one bar or point per day
Headline: From launch on 16 Sep to 22 Nov the three non-brand campaigns spent $7,191.05 on 1,394 clicks and produced 15 HubSpot leads ($479 per lead). Three findings for the 31 days since the last report (23 Oct to 22 Nov):
  1. Clicks are cheaper. 700 clicks at $4.56 each, against 694 at $5.76 in the first 37 days. The same US$100 a day now buys 20% more clicks a day (22.6 against 18.8).
  2. Leads slowed, and the paid landing pages have stopped producing them. 4 leads in 31 days, against 11 in the first 37, so the cost per lead since launch rose from $363 (to 22 Oct) to $479. The three paid pages took 320 clicks from 6 Nov and recorded no lead; the 2 newest leads came through the Customer Portal page. The paid-page forms need a test submission before more changes are judged on leads.
  3. Fewer searchers click since 14 Nov, and the new headlines are only part of the reason. Click-through rate (clicks as a share of ad views) fell from 7.0% (31 Oct to 13 Nov) to 4.4% (14 to 22 Nov). Ad views rose by 122 a day, and about half of the rise came from trucking searches matched by one Discovery ad group (BM TMS). Of the three ads edited on 14 Nov, one improved and two fell.
The Brand campaign, live since 13 Nov, is reported in its own section and excluded from every figure above: 105 clicks, $185.14, shown on 74% of brand searches, 2 HubSpot leads.

Next 30 days: plan and priorities

  1. Test the forms on the three paid pages: they took 320 clicks from 6 Nov with no lead. At the account's average lead rate (1.1%) about 3.4 leads would be expected, and zero by chance is about 1 in 32. One test lead per page confirms whether each form still reaches HubSpot.Landing pages
  2. Lead quality check with sales: 5 of the 15 leads reached MQL in HubSpot and 2 reached SQL, all from clicks up to 11 Oct; none of the 9 leads since then has reached MQL. The 16 and 17 Nov leads searched for company names ("global trans", "swift prime logistics") and may be people looking for their own forwarder's login.Measurement

  4. Bids: 23 Nov, the CargoWise dynamic group cut from US$3.20 to US$2.56 (156 clicks, $533.84, no lead since 23 Oct); read on 27 Nov. Every other bid holds until the form test and the lead check are done.Bidding
  5. Six negative keywords: m 5 continents (a logistics firm), m5c logistics, rep tools, and exact "shipping", "worldwide shipping" and "freight forwarder". Together at least 57 clicks and US$260.71 since 2 Oct, no lead.Account
  6. Ad copy, second read: the edited Forwarding ad doubled its click-through rate; the two edited Discovery ads fell. Judge them on 29 Nov after two full weeks, and keep or revert headline by headline.Ads
  7. Brand test: run to mid-January as agreed. The test answers whether total GoFreight leads rise while the ad shows on the GoFreight name, so it needs HubSpot's monthly lead totals from all sources for the same months, before and during the test.Measurement

Read: Brand searches are a weekday pattern: about 52 impressions a day on weekdays against 14 on weekends, and 96 of the 105 clicks came on the six weekdays. The budget never limited the campaign (0% lost to budget); the highest day was 16 Nov at $44.26. 291 of the 373 impressions came from the exact searches "gofreight" and "go freight". "go freight go" and "go freight corp" (16 impressions, $3.94) may be other companies; if so, they should become negative keywords. The ad misses 26% of brand searches on rank and is the first ad only 32% of the time, so a higher bid would buy more of the brand searches. The 2 HubSpot leads (20 Nov and 22 Nov) do not show whether the test works: the question is whether these people would have reached GoFreight through the organic result anyway, which the two-month comparison of total HubSpot leads answers.

Methodology and sources

Google Ads API, customer 1454804772, campaigns NS_Search | Competitor, NS_Search | Commercial | Forwarding Software and NS_Search | Broad | Discovery (non-brand), and NS_Search | Brand (reported separately), pulled 23 Nov 2026 for 16 Sep to 22 Nov 2026 (full days). Non-brand totals: $7,191.05 spend, 1,394 clicks, 15 leads; Google revises recent days as late data arrives, so figures to 19 Nov read $10.67 and 4 clicks lower than in the 19 Nov weekly report. Dates follow the account's time zone (US Pacific). Change markers sit on the date of the change in Singapore time; a change made during the Singapore working day takes effect late on the previous Pacific day. The Brand campaign went live on the evening of 12 Nov Pacific time (13 Nov in Singapore) and served 2 impressions that evening, which are included. Leads = conversions on the action "HubSpot - Lead", the only primary website conversion since 21 Sep; the account's other conversion actions are shown for reconciliation and never added. Impression share figures are Google's estimates of the searches the ads were eligible for. Search terms are the subset of searches Google reports. Regions are the searcher's physical location. Spend is in US dollars before tax. Account changes are logged with dates and reasons in the paid search sheet, OPTIMISATION LOG tab (#0 to #27); the 4 Nov keyword bid change and the 14 Nov ad changes come from Google Ads change history. HubSpot contact records were not re-checked for this report. Prepared by Novastacks.
