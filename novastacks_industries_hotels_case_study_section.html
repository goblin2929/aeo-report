// THE_GAME

Why hotel search is different

No other industry hands its search results to intermediaries the way hospitality does. The large online travel agencies (OTAs), including Booking.com and Agoda, outspend individual properties on content, links and ads, then charge a commission on every guest they intercept, including guests who searched for a hotel by name and only wanted the hotel's own website. A property that never invests in its own visibility ends up paying a commission on its own demand, booking after booking.

Google has also rebuilt the hotel search journey around Google's own hotel features. Searches such as "best boutique hotel in Singapore", "hotel near Marina Bay" or "where to stay in Singapore with kids" trigger maps, hotel packs and property panels, fed by the hotel's Google Business Profile and structured data, long before a standard organic result appears. Ranking in Google's hotel features is a different discipline from ranking a standard webpage, and most hotel websites were never set up for that discipline.

The newest change is travelers asking AI assistants to plan the whole trip. AI assistants compress hundreds of options into a handful of named properties, chosen from sources the assistants can read and verify. For that reason, Novastacks runs answer engine optimization (AEO), generative engine optimization (GEO) and search engine optimization (SEO) as one program for hotels: standard search rankings remain the foundation, Google's hotel features capture map-driven demand, and AI answer work decides whether the property exists in the AI conversations where travelers now decide itineraries.

// THE_SIGNS

7 signs your hotel business needs an AEO, GEO & SEO agency

1. OTAs outrank the hotel's official website for the hotel's own brand name.

Guests who search for the hotel by name land on Booking.com or Agoda first, so even the hotel's most loyal demand pays an OTA commission before reaching the hotel.

2. AI trip-planning answers recommend competitor hotels for stays the hotel is perfect for.

When an AI assistant is asked where to stay for the hotel's exact guest profile, such as a family trip, business stay or romantic weekend, the assistant names nearby properties and never names the hotel.

3. Each Google update removes search visibility that never fully returns.

Google core updates and AI rollouts keep reducing the hotel's impressions because the website was built for how search worked five years ago, not for how search evaluates the website today.

4. Search positions look stable but direct reservations keep falling.

AI-generated answers and richer Google hotel features satisfy travelers before travelers click, so holding the same ranking quietly delivers fewer guests every quarter.

5. The hotel's content ranks for attraction guides but never for booking-intent searches.

Articles about nearby sights attract readers who never reserve a room, while OTAs and competing hotels hold the location and stay searches that carry real revenue.

6. Nobody at the property measures AI answer visibility.

No one can report which AI assistants mention the hotel, for which traveler prompts, or which competing hotels keep winning the AI recommendations instead.

7. The hotel's machine-readable data was never set up.

Hotel, room and offer schema, amenity data and consistent property information were skipped when the website was built, so Google's hotel features and AI engines describe the hotel from third-party fragments instead of from the hotel's own website.

// FAQ

Hotel SEO, AEO and GEO: questions hoteliers ask

How is hotel SEO different from OTA visibility?

Online travel agency (OTA) visibility is rented: the hotel appears inside Agoda or Booking.com, the OTA controls the placement, and every reservation the OTA delivers carries a commission. Hotel SEO builds visibility the property owns: the hotel's official website ranking for brand searches, location searches and stay questions, feeding a booking engine where the hotel keeps the full room rate. OTA visibility and hotel SEO can coexist, but only hotel SEO compounds in the hotel's favour.

How do AI assistants decide which hotels to recommend?

AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Gemini assemble hotel shortlists from sources the assistants trust: review platforms, travel publications, structured data on the hotel's own website, and consistent information about the property across the web. A hotel whose rooms, location, amenities and guest sentiment are readable and corroborated becomes a safe hotel for AI assistants to recommend. Novastacks runs real trip-planning prompts, records which properties get named, and works backwards from those AI answers.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO for a hotel?

Search engine optimization (SEO) earns a hotel's official website positions in Google results for searches such as "hotel near Marina Bay". Answer engine optimization (AEO) gets the property cited inside AI-generated answers on ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews. Generative engine optimization (GEO) shapes how ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews describe the hotel when travelers ask open questions about where to stay. Trip planning now happens across all three surfaces, so a hotel needs SEO, AEO and GEO together.

How important is Google Business Profile for a hotel?

Google Business Profile is critical for a hotel. For most stay searches, Google shows a map, a hotel pack and property panels before any standard organic result, and the hotel's Google Business Profile powers the map, hotel pack and property panels. Accurate categories, photos, amenities, review responses and consistent details decide whether the hotel appears in the Google map, hotel pack and property panels at all. Novastacks treats the Google Business Profile as a ranking asset with its own roadmap, not a listing filled in once.

What structured data does a hotel website need?

A hotel website needs, at minimum: Hotel schema describing the property, address and geo coordinates; room-level markup for what guests can book; Offer data that search and AI engines can interpret; and FAQPage markup on pages that answer stay questions. Done well, Hotel, room-level, Offer and FAQPage structured data is what lets Google's hotel features and AI assistants read the property directly instead of relying on what OTAs publish about the hotel.

How much direct booking growth should we expect?

Direct booking growth depends on where the hotel starts. A property whose brand searches currently resolve to OTA pages has more headroom than a property already capturing its own brand searches. Direct booking growth also depends on rate parity, the booking engine experience and how competitive the hotel's market is. Novastacks measures the direct share of bookings from the first month and reports movement against that baseline instead of promising a percentage upfront.

How much do hotel SEO services cost in Singapore?

The cost of hotel SEO services is set by scope: how many properties, how many markets and languages the websites serve, the state of the current website and booking stack, and how much content needs creating versus fixing. A single boutique property and a regional group with a dozen hotels are very different engagements. Book a call, and Novastacks will scope the work honestly against the hotel's situation.

How long until a hotel sees results?

Results from technical corrections and Google Business Profile improvements often move within weeks. Results from location and content work typically compound from the second or third month. Recognition inside AI travel answers tends to follow once the property's data is fully readable and corroborated. Anyone quoting a guaranteed date for hotel search results is guessing, and Novastacks chooses not to guess.

Do you guarantee rankings or bookings?

No. Google's systems and AI engine models set search positions, and no agency controls either. Novastacks delivers and documents the work and tracks the hotel's actual rankings, presence in Google hotel features and AI answer mentions transparently, with the plan adjusted against that tracking evidence every month.

Can you work alongside our in-house team?

Yes. Working alongside a hotel's in-house team is the usual setup. Some hotels have Novastacks drive strategy, technical SEO and AI answer tracking while the hotel's marketing team handles creative and campaigns. Other hotels want everything delivered ready to publish so the hotel's web vendor only deploys the work. Novastacks adapts to the hotel's team, content management system (CMS) and approval chain.

Can Singapore grants help fund this work?

The Productivity Solutions Grant (PSG), the Market Readiness Assistance (MRA) grant and the Enterprise Development Grant (EDG) ceased on 29 September 2026. From 30 September 2026, Singapore businesses apply for grant support under Enterprise Singapore's EDGE Grant. An EDGE applicant must be a business entity registered in Singapore with at least 30% ownership by Singaporeans and/or Singapore permanent residents, and other requirements may apply depending on the activity. The EDGE Digital Marketing activity supports software costs, capped at 12 months, and the EDGE Overseas Marketing and Public Relations activity supports publicity aimed at overseas markets and does not support activities aimed at generating domestic business opportunities. EDGE support levels and cost caps differ by activity, and an EDGE application must be submitted before any work starts or any payment is made. Raise grant funding during the call, and Novastacks will walk through which EDGE activity, if any, fits the planned scope for the property.
