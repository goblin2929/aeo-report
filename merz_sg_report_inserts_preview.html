The Demand Exists. The Pages Do Not.

Merz Aesthetics is a manufacturer, not a treatment provider. That sets a real limit on how far a Merz site can go: it cannot quote treatment prices, it cannot rank clinics, and it cannot market a prescription medicine to the public the way a consumer brand can. A Merz site will never look like a clinic site, and it should not try to.

So we measured the ceiling. We pulled every Singapore search that names Ultherapy, Xeomin, Belotero or Radiesse: 159 keywords, 15,650 searches a month. Then we sorted them by what a manufacturer is genuinely able to answer. The result is the opposite of what the regulatory constraint suggests.

Read the fourth column, not the second. 10,610 searches a month, 67.8% of the market, sit in query types a manufacturer can answer in full: what the product is, how it works, what it treats, whether it is safe, and which certified clinics provide it. A further 22.3% is partly available, where the factual technology distinction can be published even though a superiority claim cannot. Only 9.9%, price and best-clinic rankings, has to be left to the clinics.

Merz is currently absent from most of that 67.8%, and the reason is not regulatory. Every one of these query types is answered today by somebody, usually a Singapore clinic or a forum thread. The queries do not surface a Merz page because the page has not been built. Sixty-eight percent of the market is available under the rules that already apply to a manufacturer, and the content development to claim it has not been done.

The Proof Is Inside the Estate

Content coverage

merzaesthetics.com.sg carries five pages for Belotero and captures 43% of Belotero search demand in Singapore. It carries one page for Ultherapy and ranks 15th for the word "ultherapy", a term searched 2,000 times a month. That single keyword currently returns zero visits to any Merz property.

The relationship is not theoretical. Where Merz has built pages, Merz ranks. Where Merz has not, clinics do. Ultherapy accounts for 86.8% of all Singapore brand demand across the four products and has the least page coverage of any of them.

Two keywords carry more than half the base case. "ultherapy" is searched 2,000 times a month and returns nothing to Merz today, because both Merz properties rank outside the click range at 7th and 15th. "ultherapy prime" is searched 1,700 times and sits 4th. Bringing those two into the top three, at the click through rate Merz already achieves elsewhere, accounts for roughly 690 of the 1,281 additional monthly visits. The forecast does not depend on winning 159 keywords. It depends on winning the two largest, then adding coverage.

The Risk We Would Flag Before Signing Anything

Migration

1,627 of the 1,862 monthly visits, 87% of all Merz organic traffic in Singapore, currently sit on ultherapysg.com, a static site with no content management system. Any decision to consolidate that content must be executed with a complete redirect map and rank monitoring, or the programme begins by destroying most of the baseline it is meant to grow. This is manageable, and it is the single item we would want agreed before work starts.
