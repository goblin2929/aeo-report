Executive Summary

Period in Review: demand normalized off last week's exceptional peak, with positions holding

Combined clicks eased to 106,365 (-21.20% vs the prior week), giving back part of last week's exceptional rebound. Importantly this week still sits above the trough two weeks ago (106,365 vs 98,890 in 24 to 30 August), and positions held or improved in every market, so this reads as demand volatility rather than a ranking loss:

  • Indonesia -13.9%, where brand actually grew +4.9%; the easing is on the games and organic side (non-branded -26.1%, PDP -33.7%) as the large game PDPs cooled on demand while holding rank (Mobile Legends position 5.6 to 5.4, Free Fire 4.9 to 5.0). Overall position flat 5.63 to 5.64.
  • Philippines -42.9%, unwinding last week's +81.5% surge, led by brand (-44.8%, about 87% of PH). The PDP page cut still grew +6.9% and the market's overall position improved 4.61 to 4.14.
  • Malaysia -3.1%, essentially flat on a tiny base, position steady.
  • The pattern is demand normalizing after an unusually strong prior week; nothing in the position data points to a page or ranking problem.
