01 Machine-readable site

The fix: make every Merz market
reachable under one domain.

All five markets served from one domain. Each gets its own localised content, and none competes with the others.

Internal linking
Distribute authority

A deliberate link structure so the regional domain passes authority to the market and product pages that need it. Without it, authority stays on the homepage.

hreflang
Six locale signals

en-sg, en-au, en-hk, zh-hk, zh-tw, th-th plus x-default. Singapore and Australia are both English on one domain. Without this they cannibalise each other.

Canonicals
One version of truth

Self-referencing canonicals with reciprocal return tags. In August 2026 the two Singapore domains targeted the same queries and declared no relationship at all.

Locale set derived from measured query language per market. Canonical finding: Merz Singapore preliminary analysis, 7 Aug 2026.

What this means for FindDoc

For a doctor directory, the same method applies
to every doctor and clinic page it publishes.

finddoc.com.sg lists 23,252 MOH-registered doctors and 5,054 licensed clinics. Each listing is a page an AI assistant could use as the source of its answer.

Machine-readable pages
Doctor and clinic facts as text

Specialty, MOH registration, clinic address, opening hours and insurer panels published as page text and as structured data (Physician, MedicalClinic), never inside images.

Merz case: the HK comparison table was a JPEG with 0 readable values (slide 04).

Question-and-answer content
Answers to factual patient questions

Pages that answer questions asked before a booking, such as which specialist treats a condition, or which clinics in an area are on the CHAS list.

Merz case: the plan ordered work by where a Merz page was least often cited (slide 06).

Being the source AI cites
Count how often FindDoc is cited

Track how often a FindDoc page is the cited source when AI assistants answer Singapore doctor and clinic questions.

Merz case: Product questions named the brand in 100% of answers and cited a Merz page in 83% (slide 06).

Within MOH rules: Singapore’s MOH rules prohibit rankings, comparisons and patient reviews for clinics. The content covers factual, sourced information only.
