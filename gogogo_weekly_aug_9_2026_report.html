Executive Summary

Period in Review: another up week, led by Philippines

Combined clicks rose to 103,707 (+19.33% vs the prior week). All the growth is genuine, not a single spike:

  • Philippines +51.8% continued its strong run: branded +55.6% with the homepage improving to position ~2, PDP +16.7% on +54.3% impressions.
  • Indonesia +12.0%, broad and healthy: Free Fire is back on top (FF PDP +17.4%) as the Mobile Legends event normalised, non-branded +12.6%, blog +19.9%, and the overall position improved to 5.30.
  • Malaysia -4.1%, a small give-back on a tiny base, though non-branded grew +6.4% and positions improved (overall 6.50 to 5.88).
  • Mobile Legends PDP title (Diamond) is live; the CTR lift on that ~420k-impression pool is the metric to watch.
  • Note (not a fix): the ID blog spasi-kosong-nama-free-fire moved with normal demand; its position held (~6.0), so it stays a monitor item, not a content problem.

What drove Indonesia +12.0%: broad demand with Free Fire as the engine

The growth is broad and led by demand, not by new rankings. The single biggest driver is the Free Fire PDP /id/p/topup/free-fire-games, up about 1,833 clicks as impressions rose from 283k to 338k while position held at 4.5. Mobile Legends (+339) and Free Fire Max (+186) followed, the blog surged +19.9%, and non-brand clicks grew +12.6%. The aggregate position improved from 5.38 to 5.30.

The click dips are demand, not ranking losses. The Roblox voucher page lost 114 clicks while its rank improved from 2.4 to 2.2, and Magic Chess lost 107 while improving from 5.5 to 5.0. Both are softer demand on pages that held or gained rank, not content problems.

URL note (so the PDP table is not misread): /p/topup/mobile-legend-malaysia fell to zero impressions this week, but its traffic moved to the sister URL /topup/mobile-legend-malaysia, which grew to 4,497 impressions. This is a URL consolidation, not a lost page.

What drove Philippines +51.8%: mostly branded demand, read non-brand for the SEO signal

The headline +51.8% is overwhelmingly a branded surge. Branded clicks rose +55.6% (14,490 to 22,544) and are about 90% of Philippine clicks, so the topline reflects brand momentum rather than an organic breakout. The SEO led non-brand grew a healthier but smaller +25.5% (2,093 to 2,626), and that is the number to watch for search progress.

On PDP (+16.7%), Mobile Legends PH added +282 clicks (impressions 17.9k to 26k) and the Mobile Legends first recharge page jumped (impressions 1,400 to 12,046, position 1.5 to 1.1) as the live PDP title work landed. Aggregate position improved from 4.21 to 3.82.

Watch: the Poppo Live PDP lost 19 clicks even though impressions rose (31k to 41k) and rank improved (3.7 to 3.4). That is a CTR softening on the page, not a ranking loss, worth a look at the SERP layout for that term.

Why Malaysia eased 4.1%: softer brand demand, while rankings actually improved

Malaysia clicks eased 4.1%, but this is a demand trend, not a ranking or content problem, and we verified it at the query level. The aggregate position improved from 6.50 to 5.88 (6.35 to 5.92 on the visible queries), and query churn was negligible (new queries carried about 182 impressions, dropped queries about 189), so the improvement is real and not a shift in the query mix. Core queries held or improved: "gogogo" 2.2 to 2.3, "gogogo my" 1.0 to 1.0, "gogo game top up" 3.0 to 2.9.

The dip is softer branded demand: branded clicks fell 5.7% as impressions on the top brand terms eased (for example "gogogo" 651 to 633 clicks, "gogogo my" 238 to 204). Non-brand clicks actually grew +6.4%. No action needed beyond monitoring.
