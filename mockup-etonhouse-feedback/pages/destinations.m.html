Choosing your programme and length of stay

Pure Mandarin School Immersion

Spend five full days learning and playing in Mandarin in any of our six cities, starting on any Monday throughout the year, except during public holidays in China.

Cultural Immersion

Spend three days learning at school and two days exploring the city with a parent on guided excursions, on selected weeks in Chengdu and Suzhou, recommended for ages 5 and above.

Length of stay

Your child can join for 1 to 8 weeks. More time turns familiarity into confidence.

Combining cities in one stay

Join us in Beijing, Chengdu, Suzhou, Harbin, Kunming or Nanjing, and continue your child's Mandarin adventure in a new city each week. Enjoy an additional 5% off weeks 2 and 3 and 10% off from week 4, for consecutive weeks booked together in one or more cities.

Learning at our EtonHouse schools in China

We believe language comes to life through experience and play. Mandarin is the language of learning, play, inquiry and communication throughout the day. Most children and educators at our host preschools are native speakers. Your child joins an age-appropriate class and is paired with a local buddy to help them settle in. Children aged 7 and 8 will join the K2 class.

9

Questions about the best place to learn Mandarin in China

− Which is the best city in China for my child to learn Mandarin?

Each of our six cities offers your child a full school day in Mandarin. For excursions with your child, choose Chengdu or Suzhou; Beijing, Harbin, Kunming and Nanjing offer school weeks.

+ What are the differences between the cities?

Each city has its own school hours, programmes and excursions. Beijing, Harbin, Kunming and Nanjing offer Pure Mandarin School Immersion and finish at 4pm, while Chengdu (9am - 5pm) and Suzhou (8am - 4pm) offer Cultural Immersion as well.

+ Can my child attend school in more than one city?

Yes. Your child's stay can be split across any of our six cities, and consecutive weeks count towards the multi-week discount.

+ Which cities offer Cultural Immersion?

Cultural Immersion is available in Chengdu and Suzhou. Each week runs once 10 children have enrolled.

+ How much does the programme cost?

Pure Mandarin School Immersion is SGD 1,750 per child per week in all six cities, including five school days, learning materials and meals in school. A Cultural Immersion week is SGD 2,350 per parent and child pair.

+ How do families arrange hotels and transport?

Each family arranges flights, hotels and daily transport. We'll suggest hotels close to each school for you to book directly.

+ Can my child join in Harbin, Kunming or Nanjing?

Yes. Your child can start Pure Mandarin School Immersion in Harbin, Kunming or Nanjing on any Monday, throughout the year, except during public holidays in China. The fee is the same, SGD 1,750 per child per week.
