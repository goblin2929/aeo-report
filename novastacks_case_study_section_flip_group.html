Flip Group

Ranking on page one and losing the click

Gogogo, Flip Group's game top up marketplace, already ranked on the first page for its product terms, but its product titles left out the in game currency people search for. We wrote a title standard, catalogued 459 product pages in one source of truth and ran an editorial programme.

In the Philippines, retitled pages earned 131% more clicks in the 17 days after the change than the 17 days before. Flip's transfer corridor pages gained 20% more clicks, May to August, on flat impressions.
