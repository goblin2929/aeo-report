Hmlet: Staging Site for hmlet.com

How hmlet.com looks after the improvements (English) · 20 November 2026 · Prepared by Novastacks · Based on: the live hmlet.com pages on 20 November 2026, the Technical Fixes for hmlet.com document, the SEO Tag Patterns for hmlet.com sheet, the Hmlet Site Structure Proposal (homepage search box and header), and Hmlet's audience groups.
In one view
  • This staging site is hmlet.com after the improvements: the homepage, About, all 5 city pages, all 23 area pages and two sample units per area. Each page is the live page in its current design with only the changes added, so you see exactly how they look on the real site.
  • Every link between these pages stays in staging, so you can click through it like the real site.
  • Every change sits in a red box. CHANGED means it exists today and is revised; NEW means it is not on the page today.
  • Hover the ? next to a box (tap on a phone) to see what the page does today, the change, why it matters, and the source.
  • The box at the top of each page, "Behind the page", shows the tags visitors do not see but Google and AI assistants read: title, meta description, canonical and language tags.
  • New on the homepage: the Apartments and Hotels search box and header from the site structure proposal. New on city pages: who the apartments are for, in the same design as the homepage section.

About this staging site

  • Made from the live pages on 20 November 2026, with Hmlet's own styles, fonts and photos. Page scripts are removed, so the map, photo slider, filters and date picker do not work here; the winter campaign pop up is hidden so it does not cover the changes.
  • Links between staging pages stay in staging. Unit cards without a staging page, the footer and other links open the live hmlet.com page in a new tab. Hotel links, the Apartments and Hotels overview pages, the Search button and page 2 and later of each list are proposed pages that are not built yet: clicking them shows what they would open.
  • The new section is on city pages only. Area pages would repeat the same text 23 times; area specific content comes with the neighbourhood pages.
  • Japanese pages follow the same changes, with the Japanese patterns from the SEO Tag Patterns for hmlet.com sheet.
