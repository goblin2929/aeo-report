FAQ

Q.What is the longest possible contract?

You can stay for a minimum of 3 months and up to a maximum of two years. Additionally, depending on the room's availability, it may be possible to renew your contract. Please contact us if you wish to re-contract.

Q.Please tell me about the rent payment methods and billing cycle.

Payment is generally accepted via bank transfer. (Credit cards are also accepted under certain circumstances.)

For monthly payments, to keep things smooth, please ensure your monthly rent is paid by the 1st of each month for the month ahead.

Q.Is it possible for two or more people to move in?

Our Studio type can accommodate up to two people. We also have Full 1-Bedroom and Full 2-Bedroom apartments available for families.

Q.Is it possible for freelancers to make a contract?

Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents can enter into contracts freely with no restrictions.

Foreigners need a valid pass that permits freelance or self-employed work.

Q.Can I have a dog or cat?

Members are welcome to keep pets, limited to dogs, cats, birds, fish, rabbits, hamsters, and gerbils, with a maximum of 3 pets per unit. An additional fee of SGD $100 per month applies. Dogs aged 3 months and older must be licensed and vaccinated.

Q.Is it possible to register my address on the residence certificate?

Yes. You will need to handle the procedures yourself at the respective government office.

Q.When will I be able to receive the keys?

To collect your keys, simply enter the provided password into the designated device or lockbox at the property. This will grant you access to both the building and your room. No staff will be present, so you can move in anytime from midnight on your move-in day.

※Please note that key collection procedures may slightly vary depending on the property.
