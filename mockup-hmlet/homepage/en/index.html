FAQ

Q.What is the longest possible contract?

You can stay for a minimum of one month and up to a maximum of two years. Additionally, depending on the room's availability, it may be possible to renew your contract. Please contact us if you wish to re-contract.

Q.Please tell me about the rent payment methods and billing cycle.

Payment is generally accepted via credit card. (Bank transfers are also accepted under certain circumstances.)

For monthly payments, rent for the following month should be paid by the end of the current month.

For contracts shorter than two months, we only accept full advance payment, but for contracts of two months or longer, you can choose between full payment or monthly payments.

Q.Is it possible for two or more people to move in?

Our Studio type can accommodate up to two people. We also have Full 1-Bedroom and Full 2-Bedroom apartments available for families.

Q.Is it possible for freelancers to make a contract?

Of course you can move in. If you are a freelancer, you will be asked to show a certificate of income and a job description.

Q.Can I have a dog or cat?

We offer a wide range of properties where you can have a small dog or cat. You can filter for 'Pet-Friendly' properties on the search page under 'More'.

Even large dogs are allowed at some properties, so please feel free to contact us. In either case, a pet fee of 110,000 yen will be charged.

Q.Is it possible to register my address on the residence certificate?

Yes. You will need to handle the procedures yourself at the municipal office.

Q.When will I be able to receive the keys?

To collect your keys, simply enter the provided password into the designated device or lockbox at the property. This will grant you access to both the building and your room. No staff will be present, so you can move in anytime from midnight on your move-in day.

※Please note that key collection procedures may slightly vary depending on the property.
