Detail pages: one pattern for apartments and hotels

Building or hotel page: /property/{id} (new page on a path already in use). Apartment unit or hotel room: /property/{id}/units/{id}/detail (1,894 apartment units today, kept exactly as they are). Hmlet tells apartments and hotels apart by ID. Every detail page shows a breadcrumb Home > City > Area > Building. Example: hmlet.com/en/property/114/units/2059/detail.

What moves into hmlet.com

Each old page gets a permanent redirect (a 301) to its new page, so visitors and the search value of the old page carry over. A redirect is only switched on once its new page is live.
