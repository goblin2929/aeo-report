Executive Summary

Period in Review: a pullback off last week's peak, led by softer brand demand

Combined clicks eased to 98,890 (-14.39% vs the prior week). This comes off last week's strong rebound, and the decline is concentrated in branded demand:

  • Indonesia -8.8%, led by brand (branded -35.3%). Indonesia eased 8.8% on brand demand; non-branded grew, led by Magic Chess

Indonesia eased 8.8% (87,446 to 79,737), off last week's rebound peak, and the dip is led by brand: branded clicks fell 35.3% (30,032 to 19,432) with the brand CTR (30.6% to 26.0%) and position (1.60 to 1.87) easing.

The organic side held up: non-branded clicks grew +5.0% (57,414 to 60,305) at a higher CTR (3.21% to 4.02%). The big PDPs eased on demand off the peak (Free Fire -8,670 clicks and Mobile Legends -2,320, though both held or improved rank), which is why the PDP page cut reads -8.3% even as non-branded grew. Non-branded is a query cut and PDP is a page cut, so they can move in opposite directions.

Where the non-branded growth came from, and a clear win: the Magic Chess Go Go PDP we optimized last week is the single biggest gainer, jumping from 699 to 6,841 clicks (impressions 17k to 92k, position 5.5 to 4.1). A Roblox and Robux query cluster ("top up roblox", "top up robux") also surged, landing mostly on the homepage (non-branded homepage clicks +62%), and the blog grew +10.6%. Together these offset the Free Fire and Mobile Legends demand dip. Magic Chess is clean proof the PDP optimization program works.
