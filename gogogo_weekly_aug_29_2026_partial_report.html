Executive Summary

Period in Review: a partial week pullback led by softer brand demand

Combined clicks eased to 86,614 (-11.48% vs the aligned prior 6 days). This comes off last week's strong rebound, so read it as directional:

  • Indonesia -4.7%, and the dip is entirely brand (branded -33.2%). The organic side grew: non-branded +10.1% at a higher CTR, blog +11.3%, and PDP held (-1.7%) with rank and CTR improving.

  • Malaysia -71.7%, on a tiny base (1,070 to 303); a 6-day partial is not readable on a market this small.
  • The pattern is brand and navigational demand softening across markets; Indonesia's organic pages held up well.
  • One caveat on these numbers: the week is partial (30 August pending). Treat as an early read, not a verdict.

Indonesia eased 4.7% on brand demand; the organic pages actually grew

Indonesia eased 4.7% (73,549 to 70,102), and the dip is entirely branded: branded clicks fell 33.2% (25,078 to 16,749), coming off last week's elevated rebound, with the brand CTR (29.6% to 25.7%) and position (1.58 to 1.88) easing slightly. Branded navigational demand can swing week to week, so this is one to watch as the full week lands.

The organic side grew. Non-branded clicks rose +10.1% (48,471 to 53,353) at a higher CTR (3.12% to 3.98%), the blog grew +11.3%, and the PDPs held (-1.7%) with their rank and CTR improving (position 4.54 to 4.30, CTR 2.63% to 3.58%). Our SEO pages are healthy; the topline dip is brand navigational demand on a partial week.
