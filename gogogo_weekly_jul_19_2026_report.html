Executive Summary

Period in Review: the promo unwinds, the core holds

Headline clicks fell to 72,550 (-41.97%), driven entirely by Indonesia (-50.78%). This is the expected give-back: last week (6 to 12 July) was inflated by the Seabank 5th anniversary and DANA e-wallet campaign, and that branded and e-wallet demand has now normalised. The right way to read the week is against a normal, pre-promo week (29 June to 5 July), not against the promo peak.

  • The promo has fully unwound. ID branded fell -72.12% ("gogogo" 39,323 to 11,528, "gogogo id" 15,920 to 1,700), the promo pages collapsed (/topup 2,424 to 145 clicks, /p/e-wallet/dana 942 to 0, /e-wallet 845 to 86), and the homepage handed back its promo lift (63,618 to 21,722, position 2.96 to 4.13). None of this is a ranking loss; it is campaign demand ending.
  • The core PDPs are ABOVE the pre-promo baseline. Against a normal week the product pages are up, not down: the PDP segment is +12.41% vs baseline, Free Fire +16.57%, Mobile Legends +3.93%. The week-over-week "-25%" only exists because it is measured against the promo peak. The underlying SEO trend on the flagship products is healthy and rising.
  • Philippines held flat and improved in quality. Clicks were essentially unchanged (-0.01%) while the homepage improved from position 2.71 to 2.09 and site CTR rose to 51.05%. A stable, healthy week.
  • Malaysia flat overall, non-branded recovering. Clicks -0.76% but non-branded +9.42% and the Free Fire PDP improved to position 5.01. On a very small base.
  • The one genuine concern is an ID blog page. /blog/games/spasi-kosong-nama-free-fire has now declined three weeks running (2,631 to 2,072 to 956 clicks, position 5.15 to 5.62 to 6.09). This is a real, sustained slip, independent of the promo, and is the priority content fix.

Weekly pattern verified: the Friday effect (13 weeks of data)

We checked whether the mid-week traffic peak is a recurring pattern by measuring day-of-week clicks over the last 13 complete weeks (20 April to 19 July). Friday is a systematic peak day in all three markets; Saturday is elevated in ID and MY but a normal day in PH. The index below is each day's clicks divided by that week's daily average (1.00 = an average day), so it is normalised for the overall trend and is not skewed by the promo week.

This is consistent with weekend gaming top up behaviour: players load game credit heading into the weekend, most strongly in the Philippines (Friday +65% above the weekly average). Practical implication for this report: because every Monday to Sunday period contains exactly one Friday, the Friday effect cancels out in week-over-week numbers, so it does not distort the comparison. It matters only if partial weeks are ever compared. This week followed the normal Friday to Saturday rhythm, with no abnormal week-over-week spike.
