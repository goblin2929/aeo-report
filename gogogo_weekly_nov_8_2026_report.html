Executive Summary

Period in Review: Indonesia steady, the Philippines down

Combined clicks were 86,959 (-8.56% vs the prior week):

  • Indonesia -0.5%, steady. Brand and non-branded both held. The Free Fire PDP grew 30.3% and the Roblox PDP more than doubled (529 to 1,319 clicks), while Mobile Legends eased on fewer searches at the same position.
  • Brand searchers now land on the homepage: gogogo.com/id-id 28,417 to 36,481 clicks, while the root gogogo.com fell from 13,514 to 5,774.
  • Philippines -35.8%. Fewer brand searches ("gogogo ph" impressions 112,579 to 80,258 at the same position, 1.3 to 1.4), and from 30 October our pages almost stopped showing for "poppo coins", "poppo recharge" and, from 1 November, "ml recharge". The pages are still indexed, and we are watching these searches in the coming weeks.
  • Malaysia -43.4% on a small base (625 to 354), the same dip pattern as the Philippines.
  • Friday 6 November brought a smaller repeat of the 23 October brand surge in Indonesia and the Philippines.

Indonesia -0.5%: steady, with Free Fire and Roblox growing and Mobile Legends easing

Counting gogogo.id, gogogo.com/id-id and Indonesian searchers on the root gogogo.com together, clicks held at 73,114 (73,452 last week). Brand held (45,784 to 45,345, -1.0%) and non-branded held (27,668 to 27,769, +0.4%). Friday 6 November brought a smaller repeat of the 23 October surge (12,545 clicks on gogogo.com/id-id that day from all countries, against 8,363 the day before), again led by brand searches ("gogogo" 4,928 against 3,336).

Free Fire keeps growing: the Free Fire PDP rose 30.3% (8,940 to 11,649 clicks), led by "top up ff termurah" (743 to 1,674 clicks) and "top up ff" on the page (3,133 to 3,759). Roblox is starting to return: the Roblox PDP more than doubled (529 to 1,319 clicks, impressions 5,322 to 11,920), and "top up roblox" grew from 81 to 339 clicks (position 10.4 to 8.8), although most of that search still lands on the coins page (see the Roblox note below).

Mobile Legends eased: the Mobile Legends PDP fell 22.4% (10,342 to 8,030). "top up ml" kept its position (8.2 to 8.1 on the page) but had fewer impressions (100,853 to 88,005), so fewer people searched for it this week; the ranking did not drop. The blog fell 24.3% (1,599 to 1,210).

Not fully recovered yet. Non-branded search is at about half the 52,293 clicks of the move week (21 to 27 September), and "top up ml" still ranks 8.1 against 6.9 before the move.

Roblox: the coins page still takes "top up roblox"

For Indonesian searches of "top up roblox" this week, Google showed the /id-id/coins page 3,670 times (position 8.9, 322 clicks) and the Roblox PDP only 83 times (position 7.3, 9 clicks). Brand searches: the homepage takes over from the root

The new homepage grew from 28,417 to 36,481 clicks (position 2.0), while Indonesian clicks on the root gogogo.com fell from 13,514 to 5,774. Together they held (41,931 to 42,255). Google is moving brand searchers onto the homepage itself, which is where they should land.
