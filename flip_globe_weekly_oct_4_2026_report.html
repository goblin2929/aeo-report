What happened to Globe: clicks up 22% with the position improving again

Globe clicks grew 22.4% (294 to 360) and the aggregate position improved from 7.14 to 6.79, a new best weekly level for the second week running. Clicks and position moved up together, so the click drivers are strengthening rather than just the long tail.

GSC caveat: most Globe impressions are anonymized, so the position is a page level figure; the combination of more clicks and a better position is the reliable read this week.

Globe position trend since July: we are in a better position now

On a monthly average basis the Flip Globe position has improved over the period we have managed it: 8.47 in July, 10.12 in August (the dip we diagnosed as the anonymized long tail broadening, not a ranking loss), and 7.42 in September. September is the strongest of the three full months and better than July, and October has started at 6.44 (1 to 4 October), so the direction is clearly positive.
