Executive Summary

Period in Review: a strong up week, led by a Mobile Legends event

Combined clicks rose to 97,729 (+34.71%) on impressions up +61.20%, with all three markets growing. The story splits by market:

  • Indonesia +31.23%, driven almost entirely by a Mobile Legends demand event. The Mobile Legends PDP jumped +242% clicks (7,059 to 24,140) on +231% impressions (302k to 999k), peaking on Tuesday 21 July (9,821 clicks that day alone). The whole ML top up category surged: "top up ml" +214%, "codashop ml" +314%, "codashop" +600%, "diamond ml" and "topup mlbb" all multiplied. Free Fire cooled in contrast (PDP -32%). Because ML positions held at about 5, this is captured demand from an event, not a ranking change.
  • The ML PDP is now the single biggest opportunity on the site. It holds roughly 1 million impressions at position 5.16 with 2.42% CTR. A push into the top 3 on this one page would convert an enormous pool, which makes the low-hanging-fruit PDP work the clear priority.
  • Philippines +44.90%, a genuinely strong branded week with ranking gains. The homepage improved from position 2.09 to 1.72 and the Mobile Legends PDP from 2.34 to 1.46; branded clicks +44.47% and non-branded +48.27%. Quality improved, not just volume.
  • Malaysia +13.88%, positions improving. The homepage improved 5.39 to 4.85, PDP +15.93% and non-branded +26.79%. Impressions grew faster than clicks (+51.69%), so CTR eased as more, deeper impressions entered.
  • One to note (not a fix yet): the ID blog /blog/games/spasi-kosong-nama-free-fire eased again (956 to 430 clicks), but its position moved only slightly (6.09 to 6.62) and impressions fell with it. Clicks and impressions down while the position roughly holds points to a seasonal or trend decline in demand for the term, not a content problem. We are noting the situation and monitoring; we would refresh the content later only if the position itself starts slipping.

The Mobile Legends event: what happened, and is it durable?

A category-wide Mobile Legends top up surge drove Indonesia this week, concentrated on Tuesday 21 July. This is worth separating from the underlying trend:

Target Keyword Performance (vs Target Rank)

How to read this

This tracks the agreed ID and PH target keywords against their target rank (Top 2, 3 or 5), from two position sources so we can cross-check:

  • GSC Pos is our own 28-day impression-weighted average (29 June to 26 July 2026), taken from the highest-impression page ranking for that query. This is actual behaviour and drives the row colour. The GSC ranking page is shown in the "Ranking Page (GSC)" column.
  • Ahrefs Pos is the best SERP snapshot position (main organic result only), carried from the 22 July 2026 snapshot (Ahrefs is refreshed less often than GSC). ">100" means Ahrefs does not have that keyword in its top 100, common for low-volume Philippines terms.

The two measure different things, so they will not match exactly, and where the two ranking pages differ it flags a page-targeting question.
