Allround Forwarding spent months evaluating logistics software. After trying seven different systems, they chose GoFreight because it was the only complete platform they could actually afford and roll out in weeks, not quarters.

After trying 7 systems, GoFreight was the only one that had everything we needed without being overwhelming.

The challenge

Why 7 systems fell short

Before GoFreight, every option Allround tested came with a catch. The team kept running into the same four walls:

  • Incomplete platforms with no built in accounting and a clumsy user experience
  • Systems so complex they needed weeks of training before anyone could use them
  • Hidden fees and per module pricing that made the real cost impossible to predict
  • Implementation timelines of 6 to 9 months before going live
The solution

Why GoFreight was different

GoFreight combined operations, documentation, and accounting in one platform the team could learn in days. Pricing was transparent and the rollout took weeks.

  • Quoting, shipment entry, and billing in one connected place
  • Learnable in days, not weeks of training
  • Transparent pricing with no per module surprises
  • Live in weeks instead of two or three quarters
The outcome

Revenue 2.6x in two years

With one connected system, Allround scaled from $10.5M to $27.7M in annual revenue while holding its team lean. Operating cost as a share of revenue fell from 3.3% to 1.7%, roughly cut in half, unlocking an estimated $459,000 in annual profit at 2025 volumes.
