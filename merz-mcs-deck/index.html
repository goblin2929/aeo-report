11 / 21

MerzAesthetics

Move one / Machine-readable

Build for the machine
that reads you.

Cloudflare’s position is that human visitors become the rounding error of the AI internet. Whether or not that lands in full, the direction is settled: the primary reader of your website is now a machine.

What the machine finds today

  • No Product or MedicalDevice schema anywhere in the estate. Nothing tells a machine that Merz makes these products.
  • Every page ships the same generic markup block — WebSite, WebPage, Organization. No product entity.
  • FAQ content exists but is unstructured — no headings, so it cannot be parsed into question and answer.

What we build

  • Semantic HTML and a correct heading hierarchy — one descriptive H1 per page, each answerable question its own heading.
  • Product and MedicalDevice schema on every product page, with manufacturer resolving to Merz Aesthetics.
  • Advisory at design stage, implementation if wanted. We work alongside your web team before the structure is set, not after.

Estate findings verified by direct HTTP request, 7 Aug 2026, Merz Singapore properties.

Move one / Reachable

Then make every market
reachable under one domain.

Five markets, one destination. Each served its own localised content, none competing with the others.

Internal linking
Distribute authority

A deliberate link structure so the regional domain passes authority to the market and product pages that need it — rather than stranding it on the homepage.

hreflang
Six locale signals

en-sg, en-au, en-hk, zh-hk, zh-tw, th-th plus x-default. Singapore and Australia are both English on one domain — without this they cannibalise each other.

Canonicals
One version of truth

Self-referencing canonicals with reciprocal return tags. Today the two Singapore domains target the same queries and declare no relationship at all.

Move three / Off-site citation

Earn the citations
you cannot own.

Merz is a manufacturer, not a provider. It cannot quote treatment prices and should not chase best-clinic lists. That is not a limitation — it defines where the leverage is.

Distribution is the citation network

  • Doctors and clinics carry their own domains and their own authority — and are already cited in the answers where Merz is absent.
  • We identify which are being cited today and which are actively promoting the brand, then build the programme around them.
  • Pre-approved, schema-marked material certified clinics publish with attribution back to Merz. Their authority, carrying your claims.

Echo Network — listening underneath it

  • Identifies who to work with, and reads how consumers actually discuss the treatment in the forums and communities engines cite.
  • Taiwan makes the case concretely: Dcard carries 1,600 monthly searches attached to this treatment — a single community shaping the category conversation.
  • Sentiment split by funnel stage, the publishers AI actually cites, and the content actions that follow.
