Executive Summary

Period in Review: a search surge in all three markets, and the blog lost most of its traffic

Combined clicks were 154,833 (+78.05% vs the prior week):

  • Indonesia +65.7%. Brand and Mobile Legends searches jumped on Tuesday 10 November, with a second rise on Friday 13 November, at about the same positions. The Mobile Legends PDP went from 8,030 to 30,118 clicks, and non-branded search (50,902 clicks) is close to the move week again (52,293).
  • Philippines +143.9%. The same surge. On Sunday 15 November "poppo coins" and "poppo recharge" showed our Poppo Live page again at the earlier level, after almost no impressions from 30 October; next week will confirm it holds.
  • Malaysia +120.6% on a small base (354 to 781).
  • The Indonesian blog lost most of its traffic (1,210 to 240 clicks) because of a server issue on the blog. The team fixed it and tested it by hand, but on 18 November every blog page we tested failed to load again. This is the first item in Next Action Items.

Indonesia +65.7%: a surge in searches on 10 and 13 November

Counting gogogo.id, gogogo.com/id-id and Indonesian searchers on the root gogogo.com together, clicks rose from 73,114 to 121,143. Brand clicks went from 45,345 to 70,241 (+54.9%) and non-branded from 27,769 to 50,902 (+83.3%). The rise came on Tuesday 10 November (23,220 clicks on gogogo.com/id-id that day from all countries, against 8,654 on Monday), with a second rise on Friday 13 November (22,214). The Philippines and Malaysia rose on the same two days. Brand positions held at about 1.1 to 1.2 and "top up ml" stayed between about position 7.8 and 8.9, so the extra traffic came from more people searching, which points to a promotion or in game event rather than a ranking change.

Mobile Legends led the rise: the Mobile Legends PDP went from 8,030 to 30,118 clicks. "top up ml" grew from 3,041 to 13,070 clicks, with impressions up from 88,639 to 376,756 at about the same position (8.2 to 8.4). On 10 November alone it had 106,157 impressions, against 13,056 the day before.

Free Fire held and Roblox kept improving: the Free Fire PDP went from 11,649 to 11,996 clicks. "top up ff termurah" had fewer searches this week (impressions 17,607 to 7,702 at a slightly better position, 5.8 to 5.6), while "top up ff" grew on the page (3,777 to 4,348 clicks). The Free Fire MAX PDP improved from position 6.0 to 4.3. The Roblox PDP rose from 1,319 to 1,668 clicks and its average position from 5.9 to 2.7 (searchers from all countries).

Non-branded search is close to the move week again: 50,902 clicks, against 52,293 in the move week (21 to 27 September). Part of this week's figure comes from the surge, so next week will show how much of it holds.

A blog server issue cost most of the blog traffic: clicks fell from 1,210 to 240

Blog impressions fell from 119,309 to 21,850. By day, blog clicks started falling on 5 November, from about 250 a day in early November to 15 to 21 a day from 12 November. On 18 November every blog page we tested, and the blog home gogogo.com/id-id/blog, waited 15 seconds and then returned an error (504 Gateway Timeout), while product pages and the homepage loaded in under a second. Google's PageSpeed test, which loads the page from Google's own servers, also failed on the blog pages and loaded the Mobile Legends PDP normally. The team traced the drop to a server issue on the blog, fixed it and tested the blog by hand, but on 18 November the blog was failing again, so the problem is not yet resolved. Google still lists the blog pages, and its own visits to eight of them on 16 and 17 November still loaded, so the blog can still win its traffic back once the server is stable after the move to gogogo.com on 21 September. Google may visit less often while pages keep failing. See Next Action Items.
