Headline: In its first five weeks the rebuilt account spent $3,991 on 693 clicks and produced 11 HubSpot leads ($363 per lead). In the five full weeks leads rose each week (1, 1, 2, 3, 4) while the cost of a click fell each week ($7.08 → $5.08). The 9 Oct landing page fix holds: 83% of clicks since 9 Oct landed on a paid landing page, up from 39% before. Competitor holds 8 of the 11 leads, Commercial 3, Discovery none, and the 4 leads since the last report all came from one ad group, the Magaya dynamic group. Google reports the search behind 7 of the 11 leads and none of the 7 reads as a forwarder looking for software, so the lead-quality review with sales is still the first job.
