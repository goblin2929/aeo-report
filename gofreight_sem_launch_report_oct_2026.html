Paid Search Launch Report: first 23 days

Google Ads Phase 1, three campaigns · GoFreight ↔ Novastacks
9 October 2026 · Thursday
Period: 16 Sep – 8 Oct 2026 (launch to the last full day) · Weekly trends in 7-day blocks from launch
Headline: In its first 23 days the rebuilt account spent $2,507 on 399 clicks and produced 4 HubSpot leads ($627 per lead). The cost of a click has fallen every week since launch ($7.98 → $5.76) after we returned the campaigns to manual bidding on 28 Sep, and weekly clicks rose from 118 to 129 on the same budget. The Competitor campaign carries the results so far: three of the four leads, all from the CargoWise landing page. Two things held the Forwarding campaigns back and were fixed on 9 Oct: its clicks were still landing on the old product page, and two keyword bids sat above the caps we set. The next month is about lead quality and landing pages, not more traffic.

Next 30 days: plan and priorities

  1. Lead quality check with sales: what happened to the four leads in HubSpot, and which searches should count as a good lead. This sets the cost-per-lead bar the bidding rule needs.Measurement
  2. Landing page copy edits from Emily's review applied to all three paid pages; then a fair conversion test of the new Forwarding page, which had no paid clicks before 9 Oct.Landing pages
  3. Replace the inherited sitelinks (Pricing, Customer Portal, Container Tracking and five more) with links to the three paid pages: 26% of clicks and $500+ went to product pages with no form above the fold.Account
  4. Weekly bid rule, CPC and cost per lead: every Friday each ad group is proposed up, down or hold from the last 14 days (20-click minimum before any verdict). First proposal: cut the Magaya dynamic group, which spent $478 on off-target searches.Bidding
  5. Country decision: Canada took 53% of clicks at a lower CPC and 3 of 4 leads, but the leads came from searches that do not read as forwarder buyers. Agree whether Canada keeps its share, gets its own budget, or is reduced.Targeting
  6. Negatives by the 5-click rule: a search is blocked only after more than 5 clicks with no lead. Watch list this month: French container-tracking searches, parcel couriers, trucking load boards.Account
Items to monitor: the dynamic Forwarding group has had no impressions since 28 Sep (page feed added 9 Oct; decision 16 Oct) · the "cargowise" keyword lost impressions when the Magaya dynamic group took most of the Competitor budget · 68–90% of eligible impressions are still lost to budget, so cheaper clicks, not higher bids, are the lever.
