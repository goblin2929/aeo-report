Paid Search Report: first four weeks

Google Ads Phase 1, three campaigns · GoFreight ↔ Novastacks
14 October 2026 · Wednesday
Period: 16 Sep – 13 Oct 2026 (launch to the last full day) · Weekly trends in 7-day blocks from launch
Headline: In its first four weeks the rebuilt account spent $3,049 on 505 clicks and produced 7 HubSpot leads ($436 per lead). Leads rose each week (1, 1, 2, 3) while the cost of a click fell each week ($7.08 → $5.33); since 28 Sep every campaign runs on manual bidding with a cap per ad group. The 9 Oct landing page fix worked: 84% of clicks since 9 Oct landed on a paid landing page, up from 39% before, and the new Forwarding page produced its first 2 leads on 11 and 13 Oct. Competitor holds 4 of the 7 leads, Commercial 3, Discovery none. Five of the seven leads came from searches that do not read as forwarders looking for software (Google did not report the other two), so the first job for the next month is a lead-quality review with sales.

  3. Replace the inherited sitelinks (Pricing, Customer Portal, Container Tracking and five more) with links to the three paid pages: Pricing and Customer Portal still took 15 clicks since 9 Oct, and 117 clicks ($650) since launch, with no form above the fold.Account
  4. Weekly bid rule, CPC and cost per lead: every Friday each ad group is proposed up, down or hold from the last 14 days (20-click minimum before any verdict). Open proposals: cut the Magaya dynamic group (bid still $4.00; $682 and 1 lead since launch) and the two forwarding keyword groups (0 leads in 14 days).Bidding
  5. Country decision: Canada took 55% of clicks at a 23% lower CPC and 6 of 7 leads, all six in Ontario, but most of those leads came from trucking and rate-lookup searches. Agree whether Canada keeps its share, gets its own budget, or is reduced.Targeting
  6. Negatives by the 5-click rule: a search is blocked only after more than 5 clicks with no lead. Watch list this month: French container-tracking searches, parcel couriers, trucking load boards.Account
