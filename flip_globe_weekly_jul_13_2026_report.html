Executive Summary

Period in Review

Within the Globe scope, the currency converter drives almost all the volume (5,664 of 5,974 clicks) and softened this week on normal FX-search seasonality, while Flip Globe grew +7.64% and the international-transfer blogs grew +12.94%. Reading each area:

  • Currency Converter is the engine, and its dip is demand, not ranking. Indonesian converter clicks eased -6.13% but average position held flat at 6.7; impressions on the big pools fell single digits as FX interest cooled. The English converter is tiny (225 clicks) and weak (position 9.4), and the largest target of all, English "usd to idr" at 1.83 million searches, still earns zero clicks at position 10. This is the biggest untapped ceiling in the whole engagement.
  • Flip Globe is trending up. Clicks +7.64% and impressions +10.69%. Priority pages Malaysia (+70%) and Philippines (+143%) grew strongly; the head term "transfer uang ke luar negeri" now ranks position 3.44. The exception is China, down -70% with its position slipping 7.93 to 9.58, the priority page to prioritise for the destination-content refresh.
  • International-transfer blogs are the quiet winner. The 121 blogs about sending money overseas grew +12.94% (to 1,850 clicks) even as the whole-site blog declined -1.13%. The SWIFT-code cluster (bri, bni, bca, seabank, mandiri) is the standout opportunity: large impression pools at 0.3% to 1% CTR and positions 7 to 10.
  • Technical finding this week: the converter hreflang tags between the Indonesian and English versions are broken (they point to a 404 and a redirect). Details and the fix are in Additional Pursuits.

Additional Pursuits

1. Optimize the priority Globe landing pages by destination context

Adapt each priority Globe page's content to its destination so the page is clearly relevant to "send money to [country]" intent: the payout methods and receiving banks or wallets used in that country, arrival times, the common transfer reasons for that corridor, and local currency context. This raises topical relevance and CTR for the priority pages (Malaysia, China, Singapore, Australia, Philippines). China is the first candidate (it fell -70% and slipped to position 9.58). Working document: Globe priority landing page optimization (Google Doc).

2. Technical fix: broken converter hreflang between the ID and EN versions

The Indonesian and English converters use different path slugs (/id/kalkulator-mata-uang/ vs /en/currency-converter/), but the hreflang alternate tags only swap the /id/ and /en/ prefix and keep the wrong path. The result: each language points to a broken URL for the other, so Google cannot pair the two versions. We verified this live:

  • On the ID page, the en-US alternate points to /en/kalkulator-mata-uang/... which returns 404.
  • On the EN page, the id-ID and x-default alternates point to /id/currency-converter/... which returns a 301 redirect (hreflang should point to the final 200 URL, not a redirect).

Example page pair: /id/kalkulator-mata-uang/idr-mys-myr and /en/currency-converter/idr-mys-myr.
