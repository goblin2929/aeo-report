Executive Summary

Example: the Free Fire page's average position (5.1) is close to September (4.7), while "top up ff" on it is 1.5 positions below September (6.7 against 5.2). Indonesia -45.7%: the surge ended, and clicks are back near the week before it

Counting gogogo.id, gogogo.com/id-id and Indonesian searchers on the root gogogo.com together, clicks fell from 121,143 to 65,785. Last week's surge in searches (10 and 13 November) faded from 16 November. Against 2 to 8 November, the week before the surge, clicks were 10.0% lower (73,114 then). Brand clicks went from 70,241 to 37,008 (45,345 in the week to 8 November) and non-branded from 50,902 to 28,777, slightly above the week to 8 November (27,769). Brand positions held at about 1.5 to 1.7 every day, so the change came from the number of searches, not from rankings.

Wednesday 18 and Thursday 19 November were the lowest days: 6,523 and 6,921 clicks on gogogo.com/id-id from all countries, against 7,464 to 8,363 a day from 3 to 5 November. Brand impressions on those two days (32,298 and 34,853) were below early November (40,470 to 48,582 a day from 2 to 5 November), at the same position.

Mobile Legends is back to about its level before the surge: the Mobile Legends PDP went from 30,118 to 8,812 clicks (8,030 in the week to 8 November), at a better position (7.3 to 6.2). "top up ml" went from 13,070 to 3,449 clicks.

Roblox and Magic Chess grew: the two Roblox pages together went from 2,554 to 4,187 clicks (see Roblox below). The Magic Chess Go Go PDP went from 959 to 2,828 clicks; "top up mcgg" went from 163 to 533 at about the same position (3.0 to 2.4), so more people searched for it.

The blog recovered after the server fix: clicks rose from 240 to 1,164

Blog impressions rose from 21,850 to 97,868. By day, blog clicks came back from 16 November (81, then 149 to 198 a day from 17 November), close to early November (178 to 250 a day from 2 to 5 November), and the week is close to 2 to 8 November (1,210 clicks). On 25 November the blog home gogogo.com/id-id/blog and the articles we tested loaded normally, in about 1 second. A stable server after the move to gogogo.com is what lets the blog keep this traffic (Next Action Items).
