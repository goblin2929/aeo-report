What the table says

Business Glossary is the only product family that converts. It takes 43% of all product clicks from 6% of the impressions — a click-through of 0.35%, roughly 10 times Metadata Management and 36 times Column-level Lineage. It is also the best-ranked line that carries real volume, at position 15. On clicks it is the largest product Decube has, and on impressions it is the fifth.

Metadata Management is the biggest and the fastest-growing, and it is not paying yet. Impressions went 32,393 → 99,333 (+207%) and its position moved from 27 to 12 — off the third page onto the first. Clicks moved 29 → 33. A single page, top-data-catalog-tools, holds position 8 on 59,100 impressions and earns nothing.

Column-level Lineage is the widest gap in the table. 71,869 impressions in 45 days, position 27 → 12, and 7 clicks. data-lineage-concepts sits at position 9 on 163,325 impressions and returns 3. Decube has won this demand in the rankings and is losing it on the result page.

Data Governance is the one line growing on clicks — 8 → 24 (+200%), on the worst average position of any product (34). The winning queries are definitional: what data governance is, what it means. That demand is real and Decube ranks nowhere near the top of it.

Data Products and Trusty AI are not in the market. Between them, one click in ninety days. Data Products has 1,066 impressions and no page ranking better than position 49; Trusty AI ranks well (position 6) but only for its own name, which nobody searches yet.

The pattern across every line: the eight products gained +77% impressions and moved from position 30 to 19, while click-through fell 0.079% → 0.050%. Rankings are being won and clicks are not following. That is a title, snippet and SERP-feature problem, not a content-volume problem — worth looking at what occupies those result pages before commissioning more articles.

Method

Search Console API for https://www.decube.io/, pulled week by week so no week is truncated by a row limit — 120,655 query rows plus 65,957 query×page rows. Brand queries, Indonesian traffic and adult/piracy spam are removed before anything is counted; the spam pattern is the one used in the monthly report plus twenty-nine new variants found in this window. Each query is tested against the L2 rows in order and the first match wins, so the specific always beats the general. Position is weighted by impressions. Scripts and raw data sit beside this file in 02_deliverables/2026-09-16_product-taxonomy-gsc-90d/. Search Console withholds rare queries, so per-query totals sit below the site totals in the interface; both halves are withheld the same way, so the comparison holds.
