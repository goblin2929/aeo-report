What happened to Globe: a softer week, position eased slightly

Globe clicks eased 18.0% (322 to 264) this partial week and the aggregate position slipped slightly from 7.30 to 7.65, after two strong weeks of improvement. This sits within normal week to week movement, and Globe remains well below the 10 to 11 range of late August.

GSC caveat: most Globe impressions are anonymized, so the position is a page level figure; on a small click base a single soft week is not a trend on its own.

Globe position trend since July: we are in a better position now

On a monthly average basis the Flip Globe position has improved over the period we have managed it: 8.47 in July, 10.12 in August (the dip we diagnosed as the anonymized long tail broadening, not a ranking loss), and 7.56 so far in September (1 to 19). September is the strongest of the three and better than July, so the direction is clearly positive. Position is a monthly average, so it compares fairly even though September is a partial month.
